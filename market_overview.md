# Branded Fit Market Overview

The US branded merchandise and swag-as-a-service market represents a $19.7B opportunity driven by rising demand from mid-market venture-backed technology companies (Series B–D, 100–500 employees) seeking to strengthen employee engagement and brand identity. Current solutions fragment the procurement process across multiple vendors—brand asset management (Brandfetch), mockup generation, production partners (Printify), and e-commerce platforms (Shopify)—forcing People Ops teams to manually coordinate integrations, approve designs, manage inventory, and track fulfillment. Branded Fit targets this beachhead segment by automating the entire workflow: ingesting company domain data, generating on-brand mockups, provisioning production, and launching a live merchandise storefront in minutes. With a serviceable addressable market of $69.3M in this segment alone (based on 3,850 qualifying companies paying an estimated $18K annually for a unified, autonomous solution), Branded Fit captures the white space between point solutions and enterprise-grade merchandise platforms that require months of implementation and dedicated account management.
