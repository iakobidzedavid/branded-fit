## APPENDIX: QUICK REFERENCE RULES

### Brand-Fidelity Validation Criteria
- **CONFIRM:** ≥4/5 average across ≥2 prospects → Brandfetch extraction is market-ready
- **REFUTE:** <3/5 average or <2/3 prospects rate ≥3 → Need manual design override or Brandfetch tuning

### Provisioning Speed Validation Criteria
- **CONFIRM:** ≥4/5 average + ≥2 prospects believe 10-min timeline → Process perception is strong
- **PIVOT:** <4/5 average + skepticism on speed → May need to slow positioning (e.g., "48-hour preview") or add proof-of-speed demo

### WTP Validation Criteria
- **CONFIRM:** ≥2/3 prospects explicitly say "Yes" to $24K → Pricing is acceptable
- **CONDITIONAL:** ≥2/3 say "Yes, but..." or suggest alt. price → Note alternative points; adjust positioning
- **REFUTE:** ≥2/3 say "No" or suggest <$12K → Pricing is too high for market; consider Starter-tier focus or ROI calculator

### Pilot Intent Validation Criteria
- **CONFIRM:** ≥2/3 say "Yes" to $4,800 Brand Drop → Customer appetite for pilots is strong
- **CONDITIONAL:** 1–2 "Maybe" → Conditions prevent commitment; address blockers (Legal, timeline, design)
- **REFUTE:** ≥2/3 say "No" → Pilot offer structure may not resonate; consider alternative (e.g., free trial, smaller pilot)
