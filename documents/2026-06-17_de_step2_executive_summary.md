## 3-Tier Segmentation (Ready for GTM)

### Tier A: High-Fit (21% of market | 480 companies)
- **Profile:** Series B, 80–200 FTE, 50–100% growth, hybrid/remote
- **Pain:** "I'm hiring 15–25 people per quarter; I need onboarding kits + recognition swag in minutes, not weeks"
- **Win Condition:** Same-day order, brand auto-extraction, BambooHR integration
- **Pricing Tier:** $6K–$18K/year
- **Sales Cycle:** 2–4 weeks
- **Win Rate:** 40–50% (high intent)
- **Expected Meetings:** 48–72 qualified conversations (10–15% reply rate)
- **ARR Contribution:** ~$315K from Tier A alone

### Tier B: Medium-Fit (41% of market | 920 companies)
- **Profile:** Series B–C, 150–350 FTE, 25–50% growth, multi-stakeholder decision
- **Pain:** "We have 3 swag vendors and no brand consistency across them; need consolidated platform"
- **Win Condition:** Vendor consolidation, reporting dashboards, HRIS integration
- **Pricing Tier:** $12K–$25K/year
- **Sales Cycle:** 4–8 weeks
- **Win Rate:** 25–35% (moderate intent)
- **Expected Meetings:** 55–92 qualified conversations (6–10% reply rate)
- **ARR Contribution:** ~$432K from Tier B alone

### Tier C: Expansion-Fit (38% of market | 850 companies)
- **Profile:** Series C–D, 300–500+ FTE, <30% growth, distributed/remote
- **Pain:** "500+ employees across time zones; one dashboard for all swag, recognition, engagement spend"
- **Win Condition:** Enterprise features (SSO, compliance), advanced integrations, regional fulfillment
- **Pricing Tier:** $24K–$50K+/year
- **Sales Cycle:** 8–16 weeks
- **Win Rate:** 15–25% (lower intent, longer approval)
- **Expected Meetings:** 42–68 qualified conversations (5–8% reply rate)
- **ARR Contribution:** ~$306K from Tier C alone

**Total Blended ARR (all tiers, 15-month window):** $1.05M

   - "What's the biggest pain in that process right now?"

   - "What are you currently spending annually on swag / employee gifts?"

   - "Do you need integrations with [BambooHR / Rippling / HubSpot]?"

   - "When would you need this up and running?"
