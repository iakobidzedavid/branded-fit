## APPENDIX: ASSUMPTION THRESHOLDS

For quick reference during aggregation:

### Brand-Fidelity NPS
- **CONFIRM:** ≥80% rate ≥4/5
- **GRAY:** 60–79% rate ≥4/5
- **REFUTE:** <60% rate ≥4/5

### $24K WTP Acceptance
- **CONFIRM:** ≥80% place $24K in "acceptable" or lower
- **GRAY:** 60–79% place $24K as acceptable
- **REFUTE:** <60% place $24K as acceptable

### Warm-Intro Conversion
- **CONFIRM:** ≥15% of Step-9 prospects → discovery call
- **GRAY:** 8–14% conversion
- **REFUTE:** <8% conversion

### Use-Case Fit
- **CONFIRM:** ≥80% identify 3+ use cases OR score ≥3/5 on multi-use scale
- **GRAY:** 60–79% see multi-use potential
- **REFUTE:** <60% see multi-use potential

### Provisioning Speed
- **CONFIRM:** ≥80% with <10 min delivery AND ≥4/5 satisfaction
- **GRAY:** 60–79% meet both criteria
- **REFUTE:** <60% meet both criteria

### Storefront Impact / NRR
- **CONFIRM:** ≥80% with recommend ≥8/10 OR estimate 3+ orders/year
- **GRAY:** 60–79% meet either criterion
- **REFUTE:** <60% meet either criterion
