## CALL STRUCTURE (45–60 minutes)

### OPENING (0–3 minutes) — Establish Credibility & Rapport

**Your Tone:** Founder energy, peer-to-peer, consultative (NOT salesy)

**Script Template:**
> "Hi [Name], thanks so much for carving out time. We just wrapped your Brand Drop Pilot, and I wanted to sit down and hear directly from you about the experience—what worked, what didn't, and what you'd want if we built a longer-term offering together. Before we dive in, is it okay if I record this? We use it just for internal notes, and I'll share the transcript with you after. Also, I've got [mention: 45 min / 60 min] blocked—does that still work?"

**Your Goal:**
- Confirm recording consent (verbal + written if possible)
- Establish this is a discovery call, NOT a sales close
- Reference the warm path: "You've been a great partner through the pilot; I'd love your honest feedback"

**Metrics to Track:**
- Did they express enthusiasm about the pilot? (Y/N)
- Tone: defensive / neutral / receptive / enthusiastic

---

### DISCOVERY SECTION (3–25 minutes) — Map Workflow & Pain Points

**70% LISTEN, 30% SPEAK**

#### Question 1: Current Swag / Merchandise Process
**Open-ended** | Target: 3–5 min response + follow-up

> "Walk me through how you're currently thinking about company swag and merchandise. What does that process look like today—who's involved, what's working, what's painful?"

**Listen For:**
- Current vendor(s): SwagUp, Bonusly, Printful, in-house, none
- Budget owner and approval process
- Timeline (quarterly, annual, ad-hoc)
- Pain points: slow turnarounds, poor quality, brand inconsistency, no customization
- Success metrics: employee satisfaction, brand perception, usage

**Follow-up probes** (if needed):
- "How often do you do swag drops right now?"
- "What's the biggest headache with your current approach?"
- "How much time does [Name] or your team spend managing this?"

---

#### Question 2: Brand Fidelity & Consistency Importance
**Open-ended** | Target: 3–5 min response + follow-up

> "Brand matters a lot at [Company]. Tell me about a time when swag or merchandise didn't live up to your brand standards. What happened, and what was the impact?"

**Listen For:**
- Whether brand fidelity is a top-3 priority (Y/N)
- Specific examples of brand failures (poor mockups, wrong colors, cheap materials)
- Impact: employee perception, customer-facing embarrassment, internal frustration
- Current brand guidelines maturity (formal doc? Figma? Mental model?)

**Follow-up probes** (if needed):
- "How critical is it that swag reflects your brand identity exactly?"
- "If you had to pick one thing about our mockups that impressed you most, what was it?"

---

#### Question 3: Speed & Time-to-Value
**Open-ended** | Target: 2–3 min response + follow-up

> "Speed matters. How fast do you need new swag or merchandise from concept to in-hand? What's your ideal timeline?"

**Listen For:**
- Urgency signals: event-driven (conf, offsite, IPO, funding round) vs. planned (quarterly)
- Acceptable delivery windows (7 days? 14 days? 30 days?)
- Current bottleneck: design, approval, manufacturing, shipping

**Follow-up probes** (if needed):
- "Have you ever needed swag on short notice?"
- "Did our 14-day turnaround surprise you?"

---

#### Question 4: Pilot Mockup Feedback (Specific)
**Open-ended + structured** | Target: 3–4 min response

> "Let's talk specifically about the mockups we generated during the pilot. On a scale of 1–5, how well did they match what you were looking for? And what, if anything, would you change?"

**Listen For:**
- Brand fidelity score (1–5)
- Specific positive feedback (design accuracy, material recommendations, color matching)
- Gaps or critiques (missing elements, wrong logo placement, quality concerns)
- Whether they showed mockups internally (team buy-in signal)

**Follow-up probes** (if needed):
- "Did the mockup process feel fast enough?"
- "How many rounds of revisions did you need, and was that reasonable?"

---

#### Question 5: Top Objections & Sticking Points
**Open-ended + soft** | Target: 3–4 min response

> "If you're honest, what was the biggest 'but' during the pilot? Anything that made you go, 'Yeah, but...' before saying yes to ordering?"

**Listen For:**
- Price sensitivity (is $24K annual a blocker? $4.8K per project?)
- Vendor lock-in concerns
- Quality assurance questions
- Team adoption / internal buy-in issues
- Competitive alternatives (what would they use instead?)
- Scope creep (do they want more than on-demand fulfillment?)

**Follow-up probes** (if needed):
- "Was there anything about the pricing or model that felt wrong?"
- "Would you want different features or functionality?"

---

### POSITIONING SECTION (25–35 minutes) — Test Growth Tier & Assumptions

#### Question 6: Willingness to Commit to Annual Growth Tier
**Structured ladder + soft anchor** | Target: 4–5 min

> "Imagine we offered a 'Growth' tier for [Company Size] like you—$24,000 a year, which includes quarterly on-demand brand-drop projects, priority turnaround (7–10 days), dedicated mockup review cycles, and quarterly strategy calls. If the model worked the way it did in the pilot, would something like that be interesting?"

**Listen For:**
- Immediate reaction (enthusiastic / hesitant / interested-but-nervous)
- Objection classification: budget, model fit, risk, alternative preference
- Annual vs. pay-per-project preference
- Willingness to commit (verbal intent)

**Follow-up probes** (if needed):
- "What would make $24K feel like a no-brainer?"
- "Is it the price, or the model that feels off?"
- "Would quarterly projects feel like enough? Or would you want more flexibility?"

---

#### Question 7: Price Acceptance (Van Westendorp)
**Structured price ladder** | Target: 5 min

> "I want to get your honest gut reaction to pricing. I'll read four price points, and I just want your immediate reaction—don't overthink it. For annual on-demand swag service like we piloted:"

Read slowly, pause after each:
1. **$2,000/year** — "Too cheap—makes you wonder if it's real?"
2. **$4,800/year** — "Feels fair, but is it worth the commitment?"
3. **$24,000/year** — "Premium tier with priority, strategy calls, more projects—worth it?"
4. **$36,000/year** — "That's a lot. Would only make sense if you really needed the features?"

**Listen For:**
- Where they balk (emotional rejection point)
- Where they say "that's fair" (acceptance range)
- Implied value perception (do they tie price to specific features?)

**Follow-up probes** (if needed):
- "Which of those felt closest to 'that's a good deal'?"
- "What would make $24K feel like a strong yes instead of a maybe?"

---

#### Question 8: Top 3 Product / Feature Requests for Year 2
**Open-ended + ranking** | Target: 4–5 min

> "We're building the Year 2 roadmap for Branded Fit. What are the three biggest things you'd want us to add, change, or improve to make this an absolute no-brainer for you?"

**Listen For:**
- Roadmap insights: design templates, vendor integrations, team collaboration, international shipping, analytics
- Frequency of mentions (if 2+ customers mention same feature → high-signal)
- Deal-breaker vs. nice-to-have distinction

**Follow-up probes** (if needed):
- "If you could only pick one, what would it be?"
- "Are any of these blockers for an annual commitment?"

---

#### Question 9: Reference Customer & Case Study Willingness
**Ask for permission + social proof** | Target: 2–3 min

> "If we close a few more customers this year, we'd love to tell the story of how you used Brand Drop to solve the problem of consistent, fast swag drops. Would you be open to being a reference? Or even a short case study—[Name] talking about the impact for 10 minutes?"

**Listen For:**
- Enthusiasm (strong yes / conditional yes / no)
- Whether they want anonymity or attribution
- Willingness to do a video testimonial (higher signal than quote)

**Follow-up probes** (if needed):
- "What would make you comfortable being a public reference?"
- "Would a 15-min recorded testimonial be something you'd consider?"

---

### CLOSING SECTION (35–45+ minutes) — NPS & Next Steps

#### Question 10: Net Promoter Score (NPS)
**Structured scale** | Target: 1 min

> "On a scale of 0–10, how likely are you to recommend Brand Drop to another founder or People Ops leader at a similar company?"

**Listen For:**
- 9–10: Promoter (high signal)
- 7–8: Passive (neutral)
- 0–6: Detractor (major concern)

**Follow-up probe** (if ≤ 6):
- "What would it take to move you to a 9 or 10?"

---

#### Question 11: Conversion Intent & Next Steps
**Soft close + commitment** | Target: 3–4 min

> "Based on our conversation, I'm hearing [summarize: price feels fair / product gap on X / model works]. Before we wrap, what would it take for you to say yes to a Growth tier contract by [target date: EOY 2026 / Q3 2026]? What's the next step from your side?"

**Listen For:**
- Clear YES / MAYBE / NO signal
- Conditions (budget approval, feature delivery, pricing adjustment, integration with X)
- Decision timeline and stakeholders
- Whether they want SOW or next discovery call

**Follow-up probes** (if needed):
- "Is budget the only thing holding you back, or are there other factors?"
- "Who else needs to sign off, and what would convince them?"

## POST-CALL WORKFLOW (15–30 min after call ends)

3. **Flag deal-blockers**: price, feature gap, team adoption, budget cycle
4. **Score brand-fidelity perception**: 1–5
5. **Document NPS**: 0–10 scale

## APPENDIX: Common Discovery Questions by Objection Type

### If They Say: "Price Feels High"
> "I hear you. Walk me through what you'd expect $24K to include for [Company Size]. What features would make that feel right?"

### If They Say: "We're Not Sure About Yearly Commitment"
> "What if we did an annual contract but with a quarterly opt-out clause? Or what if we started with two projects on a 30-day trial?"

### If They Say: "We Don't Have Budget Until Q4"
> "Totally understand. So if budget opened up in Q4, and we could start fulfillment in January 2027, would the model work?"

### If They Say: "We Like SwagUp Better"
> "What specifically does SwagUp do better for you? Help me understand so we can either match it or find a different angle where we add more value."

### If They Say: "We'd Need Internal Sign-off"
> "Who would need to sign off, and what question would convince them? Can we schedule a 20-min call with them?"

## Key Success Metrics

✅ **HIGH SIGNAL:**
- NPS ≥ 8 (promoter intent)
- Pricing acceptance at $24K tier (no objection)
- Clear YES on Growth tier conversion
- ≥2 customers cite same product gap (roadmap signal)
- Willingness to serve as reference/case study

⚠️ **MEDIUM SIGNAL:**
- NPS 6–7 (passive; needs feature or pricing work)
- MAYBE intent (conditional yes based on feature, budget approval, timeline)
- 1 major objection per customer (addressable via SOW negotiation)

🚨 **RED FLAG:**
- NPS ≤ 5 (detractor; fundamental product/model mismatch)
- 3+ cite pricing as deal-breaker (Step 16 repricing required)
- 2+ unable to commit within 6-month window (market timing issue)
- Brand-fidelity score <3 (product quality concern)
