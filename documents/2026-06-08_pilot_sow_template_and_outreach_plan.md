## SECTION 1: SOW TEMPLATE OVERVIEW

### Design Philosophy
The Branded Fit Brand Drop Pilot SOW is intentionally lightweight to minimize legal friction while protecting both parties. It is:
- **Simple:** 1-2 pages, clear language, no legalese
- **Specific:** Exact pricing ($4,800), timeline (30 days), success metrics (NPS ≥7), and conversion option ($24K annual)
- **Flexible:** Allows either party to decline conversion at Day 60 with no penalty
- **Enforceable:** Includes payment terms, liability caps, dispute resolution, and clear milestones

### Key Sections
1. **Scope:** Domain-in storefront, 50-unit mockup, 14-day fulfillment, NPS survey
2. **Pricing:** $4,800 all-in, 50/50 split payment (Day 0 + Day 2)
3. **Deliverables Table:** Clear dates and owner assignments
4. **Success Metrics:** 3 conditions (mockup approval ≤5 days, on-time delivery, NPS ≥7)
5. **Conversion Terms:** Optional $24K annual Growth Contract, decision by Day 60
6. **Termination:** Either party can exit post-Day 5 with prorated refunds; no penalty for declining annual conversion
7. **Confidentiality & IP:** Brand assets owned by customer; Branded Fit retains platform IP and anonymization rights
8. **Liability:** Capped at $4,800; standard indemnification for IP claims
9. **Governing Law:** Delaware; AAA arbitration for disputes

### Customization Approach
Each prospect SOW is **customer-specific** (Vanta, Retool, Linear versions created) with:
- Prospect name in header and signature block
- Prospect brand/stakeholder references throughout
- Customizable delivery address (placeholder in current draft)
- Pre-filled company name (e.g., "VANTA, INC." in signature block)

## SECTION 4: NEGOTIATION HANDLING

### Common Objections & Responses

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "Can you extend the mockup approval to 7 days?" | "We can flex Day 5 to Day 7, but it pushes delivery to Day 26. NPS survey may slip. Let's aim for Day 5 and escalate if needed." |
| "Our legal team wants to review the contract." | "Happy to work with your legal team. We can provide a redline template, but want to keep this under 1 page to preserve speed. Can we chat with legal first?" |
| "$4,800 is expensive. Can you do $3,500?" | "$4,800 covers all costs (Shopify, mockup design, fulfillment, shipping, NPS). It's a unit economics breakeven. We can only drop price by reducing scope (e.g., 25-unit mockup instead of 50)." |
| "We want to pay 100% upfront, not 50/50." | "We prefer 50/50 to align incentives. Full upfront increases our risk of non-approval or scope changes." |
| "Can we pilot for 60 days instead of 30?" | "60 days changes the economics and requires a separate agreement. Let's prove value in 30 days, then discuss annual contract terms." |

**Philosophy:** Protect core terms (30-day timeline, $4,800 price, NPS ≥7 conversion trigger); negotiate flexibly on execution details (approval deadline ±2 days, payment timing, design revisions).
