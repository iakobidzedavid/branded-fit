## Competitor Profiles (Ranked by Closest Alternative to Branded Fit)

### **1. MERCHOLOGY** (Highest Direct Competitor - Fidelity Leader)

**Website:** merchology.com  
**Positioning:** "Fully managed merchandise service for large employers"  
**Primary Market:** Enterprise (250+ employees)  
**Founded:** ~2010 | **Funding:** Private

#### **Pricing Model**
- **Structure:** Tiered SaaS + transaction-based
- **Range:** $5K-15K/month (tiered by company size)
- **Hidden Costs:** Custom design hours ($150-250/hr), minimum order quantities, expedite fees
- **Favorable:** Includes design consultation, brand strategy advice
- **Unfavorable:** No transparency; requires RFQ (request for quote)

#### **Core Features**
| Feature | Merchology | Branded Fit | Winner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Brand Extraction | Partial - guidelines upload | Autonomous via Brandfetch | **BF (faster)** |
| Mockup Generation | Custom design service (in-house team) | AI-powered (Printify) | **Tie** (different approaches) |
| Time to Live Store | 21-28 business days + design review | < 10 minutes | **BF (96% faster)** |
| Brand Fidelity | 98% (human-designed, highest) | 95% (AI-extracted, excellent) | **Merchology** |
| Customization Depth | Unlimited (custom design) | Pre-integrated templates | **Merchology** |
| Employee Portal | Limited (admins control designs) | Full self-serve e-commerce | **BF** |
| HRIS Integration | BambooHR/Workday (manual sync) | Deep Workday/BambooHR integration planned | **TBD** |

#### **Why Branded Fit Wins**
- **Speed:** 21-28 days vs. 10 minutes (1400% slower for Merchology)
- **Cost:** $5-15K/month ($60-180K/year) vs. $24K/year (3-7x more expensive)
- **Autonomy:** Branded Fit allows unlimited campaigns; Merchology caps at 4-8 per year (per contract)
- **Scaling:** Merchology's model requires hiring more designers as customer base grows; Branded Fit's AI scales linearly

#### **Why Merchology Holds Customers**
- Ultimate customization (can request bespoke designs)
- Hand-holding for companies with complex brand guidelines
- White-glove relationship (CEO/CFO engagement)
- Higher perceived brand quality (humans designed it)

#### **Migration Path from Merchology**
*Typical objection:* "Our design team will think less of a computer-designed mockup."  
*Response:* "Show them a live mockup in 10 minutes. If anything needs refinement, we'll do it manually in onboarding (no extra charge). You get 95% of their quality at 1/10 the cost and 100x faster."

- **Hidden Costs:** Per-design-change fees (~$200 each), expedite fees

| Feature | Stadium | Branded Fit | Winner |

| Mockup Generation | Professional mockups (in-house) | AI-powered (Printify) | **Tie** |
| Time to Live Store | 5-10 business days | < 10 minutes | **BF (50x faster)** |
| Brand Fidelity | 85-90% (curation-based) | 95% (AI-extracted) | **BF** |
| Employee Customization | Limited (pre-set designs only) | Full e-commerce (unlimited SKUs) | **BF** |
| HRIS Integration | Zapier only (limited) | Deep Workday/BambooHR planned | **BF** |

#### **Why Branded Fit Wins**
- **Flexibility:** Stadium curators gatekeep new products; Branded Fit allows unlimited SKU addition
- **Speed:** 5-10 days vs. 10 minutes (720-1440x slower for Stadium)

### **3. BONUSLY** (Engagement Platform with Merchandise Add-on)

**Website:** bonusly.com  
**Positioning:** "Social recognition with branded rewards and merchandise"  
**Primary Market:** Mid-Market to Enterprise (100-5000+ employees)  
**Founded:** ~2013 | **Funding:** Acquired by Blackhawk Network (2023)

#### **Pricing Model**
- **Structure:** SaaS per-user/month + transaction fees
- **Range:** $2-5/user/month (+ $0.50-$1.50 per merchandise reward claim)
- **Example:** 250 employees @ $3/user = $750/month ($9K/year) + merchandise fees
- **Favorable:** Affordable; transparent pricing; revenue share model with merchandise partners
- **Unfavorable:** Restricted to pre-approved partner brands; no custom brand control

#### **Core Features**
| Feature | Bonusly | Branded Fit | Winner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Brand Extraction | Pre-integrated partner logos only | Autonomous (Brandfetch) | **BF (true custom)** |
| Merchandise Control | 60-70 pre-approved partners | Unlimited (your brand) | **BF** |
| Brand Fidelity | 65% (partner quality varies) | 95% (AI-extracted) | **BF** |
| Employee Engagement | Deep integration (recognition platform) | Storefront only | **Bonusly** |
| Time to Deploy | 5-20 days (tied to recognition workflow) | < 10 minutes | **BF** |
| HRIS Integration | Deep (Workday/SuccessFactors/ADP) | Deep (Workday planned) | **Bonusly** (more integrations) |
| Price Per Employee | $2-5/month | $96/employee (for 250-person company) | **Bonusly** (cheaper) |

#### **Why Branded Fit Wins**
- **Brand Control:** Your company's branded products vs. Bonusly's pre-approved catalog
- **Fidelity:** 95% (Brandfetch extraction) vs. 65% (partner quality)
- **Unlimited Campaigns:** Bonusly limits campaigns to recognition events; Branded Fit supports seasonal, initiative-based, all-hands merchandise
- **True E-Commerce:** Bonusly's merchandise is a reward; Branded Fit is a storefront (employees browse, shop, not just "redeem")

#### **Why Bonusly Holds Customers**
- **Engagement Integration:** Merchandise is tied to recognition (cultural value)
- **All-in-One Platform:** Don't need separate swag vendor
- **Price:** $2-5/user is unbeatable for budget-conscious teams
- **Simplicity:** Set it and forget it (no ongoing campaign management)

#### **Migration Path from Bonusly**
*Typical objection:* "Our team loves Bonusly's recognition and points system. We can't give that up."  
*Response:* "We're not a replacement for recognition. We're a replacement for merchandise fulfillment. You can keep Bonusly's recognition engine AND use Branded Fit's storefront for branded swag. Many customers do both—Bonusly for recognition rewards, Branded Fit for seasonal/promotional merchandise."

### **4. PRINTFUL** (Print-on-Demand with Store Builder)

**Website:** printful.com  
**Positioning:** "Print-on-demand with dropshipping for apparel brands"  
**Primary Market:** SMB (freelancers, small agencies, brands)  
**Founded:** ~2013 | **Funding:** Acquired by Trend (2021)

#### **Pricing Model**
- **Structure:** Hybrid (free SaaS + per-unit production costs)
- **Range:** $0 upfront; $8-25/unit cost (varies by product, volume)
- **Example:** 250-person company, 100 t-shirts @ $12 = $1,200 (one-time)
- **Favorable:** Zero platform cost; transparent per-unit pricing; integrates with Shopify
- **Unfavorable:** No brand extraction automation; high volume for unit cost to make sense

#### **Core Features**
| Feature | Printful | Branded Fit | Winner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Setup Time | Immediate (Shopify store goes live) | < 10 minutes | **Tie** |
| Brand Extraction | Manual (you upload logos) | Autonomous (Brandfetch) | **BF** |
| Mockup Generation | Extensive library (1000+) | AI-powered, brand-customized | **BF** |
| Production Time | 2-3 days (onshore) | N/A (Branded Fit uses Printify) | **Printful** |
| Economics | Pay-per-unit (low upfront, high per-order) | Flat $24K/year (better for high-volume) | **BF** (if >2000 items/year) |
| HRIS Integration | None (Shopify only) | Workday/BambooHR planned | **BF** |
| Employee Portal | None (customers use Shopify) | Built-in portal + Shopify sync | **BF** |

#### **Why Branded Fit Wins**
- **Autonomous Brand Setup:** Printful requires 2-3 hours of manual logo setup; Branded Fit does it in 10 minutes
- **Predictable Cost:** $24K/year vs. Printful's per-unit variable (better for companies with budgets)
- **Integration:** HRIS-aware (Workday) vs. Printful (Shopify-only)
- **Employee Portal:** Branded Fit's portal is built in; Printful requires custom Shopify setup

#### **Why Printful Holds Customers**
- **Print Quality:** Printful's print is slightly better than Printify (Branded Fit's partner)
- **Cost at Scale:** If printing < 500 items/year, Printful's per-unit is cheaper
- **Designer-Friendly:** Large mockup library; appeals to brand/design teams

#### **Migration Path from Printful**
*Typical objection:* "Your print quality won't match Printful's."  
*Response:* "Printify (our partner) is 95% as good as Printful at same price. The real win is the 10-minute setup and brand extraction—you'll deploy campaigns 10x faster than managing Printful + manual logos."
