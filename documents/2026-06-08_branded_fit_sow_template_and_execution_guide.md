#### II. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Branded Fit will design, provision, and optimize an autonomous, AI-driven merchandise storefront for [Client Company Name], powered by real-time brand extraction (Brandfetch), production integration (Printify), and e-commerce hosting (Shopify). This pilot validates:

- **Brand extraction accuracy** via Brandfetch API (target: ≥95%)
- **Speed-to-market** via Printify + Shopify provisioning (target: <10 minutes)
- **Storefront visual fidelity** via NPS-based rating (target: ≥4/5)
- **Fulfillment delivery** of 50-unit onboarding kit (target: ≤14 days)
- **Customer satisfaction** via NPS survey (target: >7 NPS score)

If 2 or more success metrics are met AND Client reports >7 NPS, Client may expand to Branded Fit's annual Growth contract at $24,000 (auto-expansion clause).

---

#### III. SCOPE OF WORK

**Phase 1: Domain Intake & Brand Extraction (Days 1-2)**
- Receive [Client Company Name] domain and brand guidelines
- Run Brandfetch API extraction on company domain, website, logo library
- Extract primary brand colors (hex), logo files, brand voice keywords
- Deliver extracted brand charter to Client for review & approval
- **Deliverable:** Brand Charter Document (JSON + visual kit)

**Phase 2: Storefront Design & Provisioning (Days 3-5)**
- Design autonomous storefront template using extracted brand colors, logo, messaging
- Create mockups for [5-10] hero products (e.g., branded t-shirts, hoodies, mugs, hats)
- Integrate with Printify's on-demand production API
- Configure Stripe checkout for seamless payment flow
- Set up Shopify hosting + domain routing
- Deliver storefront preview link to Client for feedback
- **Deliverable:** Live Storefront Preview + Stripe Checkout Test Link

**Phase 3: 50-Unit Onboarding Kit Fulfillment (Days 5-14)**
- Client selects [5] hero products and quantities for physical onboarding kit
- Provider coordinates with Printify for on-demand production
- Arrange fulfillment to Client office (domestic shipping included; international +$250)
- Track delivery with Printify + courier integration
- Deliver tracking link + expected delivery date
- **Deliverable:** Physical 50-Unit Onboarding Kit + Delivery Confirmation

**Phase 4: Analytics & Optimization (Days 15-30)**
- Instrument storefront with daily event tracking (domain submission, brand extraction, storefront generation, product views, cart adds, checkouts, orders)
- Build daily analytics dashboard (conversion funnel, time-series, event counts)
- Provide daily optimization recommendations (product mix, pricing, messaging)
- Conduct weekly check-ins with Client to assess performance
- **Deliverable:** Daily Analytics Dashboard + Weekly Optimization Reports

**Phase 5: Pilot Completion & Decision Support (Day 30)**
- Deliver final pilot summary report with success metrics scorecard
- Facilitate NPS survey with Client stakeholders
- Present go/no-go recommendation for annual Growth contract expansion
- Schedule expansion call if metrics are met
- **Deliverable:** Final Pilot Report + NPS Results + Expansion Decision

#### IV. SUCCESS METRICS

Provider will consider the pilot **successful** if the following metrics are achieved by **Pilot Completion Date**:

| Metric | Target | Measurement Method | Weight |
|--------|--------|-------------------|--------|
| **Brand Extraction Accuracy** | ≥95% | Brandfetch API outputs validated against original brand guidelines | 33% |
| **Provisioning Time** | <10 minutes | Time from domain submission to live storefront (Brandfetch → Printify → Shopify integration) | 33% |
| **Storefront Visual Fidelity** | ≥4/5 (NPS-style) | Client stakeholder rating of brand colors, logo placement, mockup accuracy, pricing display | 33% |
| **Fulfillment Delivery** | ≤14 days | Physical onboarding kit delivery time from order to Client office | Pass/Fail (prerequisite) |
| **Customer Satisfaction (NPS)** | >7 NPS | Post-pilot survey with Client team (e.g., "How likely to recommend to peers?") | Expansion qualifier |

**Conversion Criteria to Annual Growth Contract:**
- If **2 or more of the 3 primary metrics (Brand Accuracy, Provisioning Time, Storefront Fidelity) are met**, AND
- Client reports **>7 NPS score**,
- Provider will automatically offer expansion to annual Growth contract at **$24,000/year** (automatic 5x revenue expansion).

## APPENDIX: DEFINITIONS & GLOSSARY

**Brand Charter:** JSON document + visual kit (colors, logos, keywords) extracted from company domain and brand guidelines  
**Storefront:** Autonomous e-commerce site hosted on Shopify, powered by Brandfetch brand extraction and Printify production integration  
**Provisioning Time:** Time elapsed from domain submission to fully live, checkout-enabled storefront  
**Onboarding Kit:** 50 physical units of 5 hero products, fulfillment handled by Printify, delivery to Client office  
**Analytics Dashboard:** Real-time view of conversion funnel, time-series metrics, and daily event counts (brand extraction, storefront generation, product views, orders)  
**NPS Score:** Net Promoter Score—single-question survey ("How likely to recommend?") on 0-10 scale; >7 is "promoter" threshold  
**Growth Contract:** Annual ($24K/year) expansion offer with full storefront management, product expansion, advanced analytics, dedicated support
