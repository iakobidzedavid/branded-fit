# Brand Drop Pilot Outreach Email Templates

## Overview
These templates are designed for warm outreach to Step-9 prospects with the Brand Drop Pilot offer ($4,800). Each variant embeds validation questions directly in the copy to collapse the feedback cycle and increase response quality. All templates follow the principle: **make the ask explicit and low-friction**.

**Success Criteria**: ≥3 qualified responses + ≥1 discovery call booked  
**Timeline**: 3-day execution window  
**Call-to-Action**: Live MVBP demo link or 15-min discovery call booking

## Guidelines for Personalization

### Required Fields (Before Sending):
- `[Prospect First Name]` — Use their first name from CRM or LinkedIn
- `[Company Name]` — Their actual company name
- `[PROSPECT_ID]` — Unique ID for demo tracking (enables analytics)
- `[Industry]` — Their industry (tech, finance, healthcare, etc.)
- `[Competitor Name]` — Only for Template 3; use real competitor if known
- `[Objection]` — Only for Template 7; fill based on actual response

### Tone Calibration:
- **For VPs/Directors (larger orgs):** Use Templates 2, 5, 7 (data-driven, low-barrier)
- **For Founders/CEOs (smaller orgs):** Use Templates 1, 4 (relationship-first, candid)
- **For Cold Outreach:** Use Templates 2 or 5 (low-risk ask, demo-focused)
- **For Warm Intros:** Use Templates 1 or 4 (higher confidence, personal touch)

### Validation Questions (Embedded by Design):

Each template includes a specific validation question designed to:
1. **Unblock decision-making** — Give prospect clear, low-effort ask
2. **Enable rapid analysis** — Simple 1-5 rating captures key assumption (brand fidelity ≥85%)
3. **Create feedback loop** — Reply becomes data point for assumption validation

**Key Assumptions Embedded:**
- **Brand Fidelity**: "Does brand accuracy look right?" (Target: ≥8/10 rate as accurate)
- **Use Case Fit**: "Would you move forward with a pilot?" (Target: ≥50% convert to call)
- **Price Signal**: $4,800 explicit in all templates (validates WTP assumption)

## Response Tracking & Interpretation

### Track These Metrics:
1. **Email Open Rate** — Success: ≥25%
2. **Demo Click-Through** — Success: ≥15% of opens
3. **Demo View Time** — Success: >60 seconds (indicates real engagement)
4. **Validation Rating Response** — Success: ≥50% reply with 1-5 rating
5. **Discovery Call Booked** — Success: ≥1 per 10 prospects (10% conversion)

### Decision Criteria (by June 11):
- **GO**: ≥3 responses + ≥1 call booked → Proceed to discovery phase
- **PIVOT**: 2 responses, mixed ratings → Revise messaging, re-segment audience
- **NO-GO**: <2 responses → Pause outreach, conduct post-mortem

## Common Responses & How to Handle

### If Prospect Says "Not interested":
**Response Template:**
"Got it—totally understand. Just curious: is it the timing, or do you not see branded merch as valuable for [Company Name]? Happy to circle back if things change."

### If Prospect Rates Brand Accuracy 1-2 (Low):
**Response Template:**
"Thanks for the honest feedback. What would need to be different to hit a 4-5? We can adjust [colors/design/product mix] before moving forward."

### If Prospect Rates Brand Accuracy 4-5 (High):
**Response Template:**
"Awesome—glad the brand comes through. Ready to move forward with the $4,800 pilot? Let me send you a one-pager and we can lock in next steps."

### If Prospect Books Call Directly:
**Prep Points:**
- Confirm demo was compelling (which SKU resonated most?)
- Understand use case (employee gifts? Recruiting tool? Revenue stream?)
- Address any lingering brand accuracy concerns
- Walk through pilot timeline: Design approval (3 days) → Inventory setup (5 days) → Live launch (2 days)
- Close: Proposal email + contract + next milestone

## Notes for Sales Team

1. **Authenticity First**: These templates are scaffolds, not scripts. Customize with real company names, actual insights about their business, and genuine enthusiasm.

2. **Timing Matters**: Avoid Mondays (inbox overload) and Fridays (low engagement). Best windows: Tue-Thu, 9-11 AM in prospect's timezone.

3. **Watch for Engagement Patterns**: If someone clicks demo but doesn't reply with a rating, they're interested but need a lower-friction ask. Try: "Thoughts on the mockup? No pressure—just yes/no."

4. **Build Momentum**: Each response is a win. Log it immediately in CRM, update the campaign tracker, and celebrate small wins (clicks, opens, ratings) alongside big wins (calls booked).

5. **Adapt Based on Data**: If Template 2 is outperforming Template 1 by 2x, pivot the remaining outreach. This is a test—learn and adjust.
