## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

This document provides a complete framework for executing discovery calls with Branded Fit prospects. The process operationalizes the 6-experiment validation test plan (Step 20-21) by embedding hypothesis testing directly into customer conversations. Each call follows a 7-part structure designed to:

1. **Build rapport** and frame the conversation around customer pain
2. **Establish baseline** current state and constraints
3. **Demo/present** live MVBP with Brandfetch → Printify → Shopify flow
4. **Embed 4 validation questions** (brand fidelity, provisioning speed, WTP signal, pilot intent)
5. **Discover objections** and feature gaps
6. **Close** with explicit next-step commitment
7. **Document findings** in call tracker for synthesis

## PART 2: 7-PART DISCOVERY CALL SCRIPT

**Duration:** 30 minutes  
**Format:** Zoom/video call (required for screen sharing and rapport)  
**Recording:** Obtain explicit verbal consent at beginning: *"I'd like to record this call for our notes and quality assurance. Is that okay?"*

### **PART 2A: OPENING & RAPPORT (2 minutes)**

**Opener (Your lines):**
> "Hi [Name], thanks so much for taking the time to chat. I'm excited to show you something we've been building at Branded Fit that I think is going to solve a real problem for you. But before I jump in, I'd love to understand your world a bit better. What's your biggest headache with company merchandise right now?"

**Listen for:** Job pains (disengagement, brand misalignment, logistics), budget constraints, frequency of orders, current vendor friction.

**Rapport building (active listening):**
- Nod, take notes visibly
- Repeat back: *"So you're saying the issue is [X]—is that right?"*
- Show empathy: *"Yeah, that's a problem we hear from a lot of teams."*

---

### **PART 2B: BASELINE & CONSTRAINTS (4 minutes)**

**Discovery questions (ask in conversational order, not rote):**

1. **Current state:** *"How often do you currently buy swag or branded merch for employees?"*
   - Listen for: frequency (quarterly, annual), budget allocation, who approves purchases

2. **Process pain:** *"Walk me through what happens today when you want to order branded items. Where does the process break down?"*
   - Listen for: vendor hunting, design collaboration, QA, shipping delays, cost surprises

3. **Brand requirements:** *"How important is it that the merch actually matches your company brand and colors?"*
   - Listen for: "Very" → strong brand/design sensitivity, good fit for Brandfetch validation

4. **Stakeholders:** *"Who else is involved in these decisions? Is it just you, or do you coordinate with design/marketing?"*
   - Listen for: approval bottlenecks, cross-functional alignment needs

5. **Budget reality:** *"If you could outsource all of this and it just worked, what would be a fair annual spend?"*
   - Listen for: WTP signal (e.g., "$10K–$30K" or "no budget" = objection)

---

### **PART 2C: PAIN VALIDATION & DEMO TRANSITION (1 minute)**

**Bridge statement:**
> "Okay, so the main issues are [summarize: e.g., 'you're spending 20 hours on vendor coordination, brand colors never match, and it's hard to know what employees actually want']. That's exactly what we built Branded Fit to solve. I want to show you how this works. Can I share my screen?"

**Await yes.**

### **PART 2D: LIVE MVBP DEMO & BRAND-EXTRACTION VALIDATION (8 minutes)**

**Setup:** Screen-share your domain on live Branded Fit Command Console  
**Narrative:** Walk through the flow as you perform it in real-time.

**Step 1: Domain input (1 min)**
> "So here's how it works. You give us your company domain—in this demo, I'm using [demo domain]. I click 'Generate,' and in the background, our AI is extracting your brand."

*Enter domain, click submit, wait for extraction to begin.*

> "What's happening right now: we're pulling your logo, brand colors, fonts, and imagery from your website using something called Brandfetch. All automated."

---

**VALIDATION QUESTION #1: BRAND-FIDELITY PERCEPTION [INSERT HERE]**

*As Brandfetch extracts colors/logos, pause and ask:*

> "**As you watch this happen, on a scale of 1–5, how accurate do you think this brand extraction is? 1 = 'totally off' to 5 = 'nails it.'**"

**Record response:** [prospect rating: 1-5]  
**Listen for hesitation:** If <4, probe: *"What's missing? What would make it perfect?"* → objection/feature request

**Note to facilitator:** This directly validates Assumption #1 (brand-extraction fidelity). Target: ≥4 ratings from ≥2/3 prospects → CONFIRM. <3 from majority → REFUTE, pivot to manual design input option.

---

**Step 2: Storefront generation (4 min)**
> "Once extraction is complete, our system auto-generates a live storefront. Employees can come here, see branded merch options—hoodies, t-shirts, hats—all in your colors and with your logo. And here's the kicker: it's all integrated with Shopify in the backend, so inventory, payments, and shipping are automatic."

*Click through generated storefront, show product preview, click on an item to show modal.*

> "This entire setup took us 10 minutes. No design, no vendor emails, no back-and-forth."

---

**VALIDATION QUESTION #2: 10-MINUTE PROVISIONING PERCEPTION [INSERT HERE]**

*After showing the live storefront:*

> "**I just want to check something: seeing that we generated a full branded storefront in 10 minutes, how realistic does that feel as a solution to your current process? On a scale of 1–5, where 1 is 'not believable' and 5 is 'that would be a game-changer.'**"

**Record response:** [prospect rating: 1-5]  
**Listen for skepticism:** If <4, ask: *"What would need to be true for this to actually work for you?"* → identifies constraints (e.g., need custom fonts, legal review required, IT integration concerns)

**Note to facilitator:** This validates Assumption #2 (10-min provisioning perception). Target: ≥4 from ≥2/3 → CONFIRM.

---

**Step 3: Pricing & Growth Tier positioning (2 min)**

### **PART 2E: WILLINGNESS-TO-PAY VALIDATION (3 minutes)**

**VALIDATION QUESTION #3: $24K WTP SIGNAL [INSERT HERE]**

> "**So here's my direct question: if Branded Fit eliminates your vendor coordination, guarantees brand accuracy, and gets a new storefront live in 10 minutes, would $24K a year—that's $2,000 a month—be something you'd be willing to commit to?**"

**Record response:**
- [ ] **Yes, $24K is doable** (explicit WTP signal) → Move to Pilot Question
- [ ] **Yes, but only if [condition]** (conditional WTP) → Record condition
- [ ] **Too expensive, we'd need to see ROI** (price resistance) → Record objection
- [ ] **I'd need to talk to [stakeholder]** (approval required) → Record blocker
- [ ] **No, doesn't fit our budget** (hard no) → Record and move to Pilot Question

**If hesitation on price, probe:**
> "What price point would feel right to you?"
> "What would you need to see happen to justify $24K?"
> "Is it the annual commitment, or the monthly number?"

**Record exact response for synthesis.**

### **PART 2F: PILOT & NEXT STEPS (3 minutes)**

**VALIDATION QUESTION #4: PILOT INTENT [INSERT HERE]**

> "Here's what I'm thinking: rather than committing to Growth tier right now, why don't we run a **Brand Drop pilot** with you? That's a one-time, fully branded merchandise drop for a specific team or event. It costs $4,800, takes us 10 days to launch, and you own all the data. If it works, you're ready to go all-in on Growth. If it doesn't, you've learned something. Does that feel like a reasonable next step?"

**Record response:**

- [ ] **Maybe, depends on [X]** → Record condition
- [ ] **Not right now, but maybe later** → Record objection

**If yes to pilot:**
> "Great. Here's what happens next: I'll send you a Pilot Agreement and a brand brief. You'll give us your brand files, pick your product mix—hoodies, t-shirts, hats—and we'll generate a storefront preview within 48 hours. You approve, we launch, and in 10 days you've got merchandise in employees' hands. Sound good?"

*Confirm next steps:*

## PART 5: GO/PIVOT/NO-GO DECISION CRITERIA

**Decision point:** After ≥3 calls completed

### CONFIRM (Go forward to Pilot)
**Success threshold met if:**
- ✓ ≥2 calls show explicit pilot intent ("Yes, let's run $4,800 Brand Drop pilot")
- ✓ ≥2 calls show $24K WTP signal (even if conditional)
- ✓ Brand-fidelity perception ≥4/5 from ≥2 prospects
- ✓ 10-min provisioning perception ≥4/5 from ≥2 prospects
- ✓ <2 critical blockers (e.g., legal review prevents all 3)

**Action:** Immediately execute Pilot Agreements with interested prospects; set pilot launch dates.

### PIVOT (Modify offer or messaging)
**Threshold met if:**
- ⚠ Only 1 pilot-intent signal (need stronger hook or different ICP)
- ⚠ Brand-fidelity <4/5 average (need Brandfetch tuning or manual design option)
- ⚠ Price resistance across all 3 (need Starter tier push or ROI calculator)
- ⚠ 2+ critical blockers (need Legal/IT value props)

**Action:** 
- Refine discovery call script based on objection themes
- Target different buyer persona (e.g., CMO instead of VP People)
- Create ROI calculator to address price objections
- Add manual design override option to address brand-extraction concerns
- Re-run 2–3 calls with revised pitch

### NO-GO (Pause or pivot business model)
**Threshold met if:**
- ✗ 0 pilot-intent signals across 3 calls
- ✗ ≥2 "No" responses to $24K WTP
- ✗ Average brand-fidelity <2/5 (Brandfetch too inaccurate for market)
- ✗ ≥2 fundamental business model blockers (e.g., "can't do automated for our use case")

**Action:**
- Pause outreach; conduct root-cause analysis on feedback
- Assess: Is ICP wrong, messaging wrong, or product wrong?
- Decide: Pivot to different persona, different use case (e.g., events vs. continuous), or rebuild product

## APPENDIX E: TRAINING SCRIPT FOR FACILITATION TEAM

**Before first call, brief team on script flow:**

1. **Don't memorize; internalize flow.** The script is a guide, not a crutch. Let conversation breathe.
2. **Listen >50% of the time.** Prospect talks, you take notes. Ask clarifying Qs.
3. **Validation questions are mandatory.** Don't skip or modify the 4 Qs. Say them verbatim or very close.
4. **Record ratings immediately.** When prospect gives 1–5 rating, confirm: *"So you're saying a 3/5, right?"*
5. **Objection capture is critical.** If prospect raises concern, say: *"Tell me more about that"* and write down exact quote.
6. **Close strong.** Always confirm: *"So next steps are: I send you the Pilot Agreement, and we schedule a kick-off for [DATE]. Does that work?"*
7. **Debrief post-call.** Spend 10 min filling out tracker while fresh. Don't wait till evening.
