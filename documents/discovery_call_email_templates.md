# Discovery Call Email Templates & Communication Playbook

**Purpose:** Schedule and conduct 30-45 minute discovery calls with warm-outreach respondents to validate WTP at $24K, objections, use-case fit, and competitive positioning.

**Document Date:** 2026-06-08  
**Campaign:** Brand Drop Pilot Discovery Calls  
**Target:** 5+ calls completed with 2-3 verbal pilot commitments

---

## Overview

This document provides 5 production-ready email templates and communication scripts for:
1. **Initial Discovery Call Invitation** – First outreach to warm leads
2. **Call Confirmation & Pre-Call Brief** – 24 hours before scheduled call
3. **Post-Call Follow-Up (WTP Signal Yes)** – For prospects with strong price signal
4. **Post-Call Follow-Up (Conditional/Hesitant)** – For price-sensitive or feature-gated prospects
5. **Pilot Commitment Formalization** – To convert verbal yes into pilot enrollment

Each template includes:
- Clear subject lines
- Personalization placeholders
- Call-to-action (CTA)
- Tone guidance
- Optional variables and fallback language

## Template 1: Initial Discovery Call Invitation

**Subject:** Let's talk about your onboarding process ([Prospect First Name])  
**Send:** Immediately after warm intro or response to outreach campaign  
**Tone:** Conversational, consultative, value-first  

---

### Body

Hi [Prospect First Name],

Thanks for the quick response—I appreciate you taking the time to chat about [Company Name]'s onboarding challenges.

We've been working with People Ops teams at companies like [Similar Company 1] and [Similar Company 2] to speed up their employee onboarding. Most of them are spending 4-8 weeks on the logistics (brand kit creation, vendor coordination, quality reviews), even after the employee starts.

I'd love to walk through your current process for 30-40 minutes and see if there's a fit. Here's what I'm curious about:

1. **How long** does your current onboarding-kit process take from brief to delivery?
2. **What frustrates you most** about the current workflow? (cost, timeline, brand compliance, vendor coordination, etc.)
3. **How does 10 minutes to live storefront** compare to your current timeline?

No pressure—if this isn't the right time, I totally get it. But if you're open to a quick call, I'm flexible on timing.

**[Pick a time that works best for you →](https://calendly.com/[your-calendly-link])**

Or feel free to reply with a few times that work, and I'll confirm.

Thanks,  
[Your Name]  
[Your Title], Branded Fit  
[Phone] | [Email]

---

### Personalization Notes
- Replace `[Similar Company 1]` and `[Similar Company 2]` with actual customer names or well-known peers in their industry (e.g., "tech startups" or "Fortune 500 HR teams")
- Use the specific pain point they mentioned in their reply email (if applicable)
- Keep email **under 150 words** for mobile readability
- Calendly link is lower friction than back-and-forth email threading

## Template 4: Post-Call Follow-Up (Conditional / Price-Sensitive / Feature-Gated)

**Subject:** A few thoughts on your constraints (and how we can work with them)  
**Send:** Within 4 hours of call completion  
**Tone:** Problem-solving, collaborative, removes perceived obstacles  

---

### Body

Hi [Prospect First Name],

Thanks for the honest feedback on the call. I totally get the hesitation on [stated objection, e.g., "timeline," "budget," "feature X"].

Here's what I'm thinking based on what you said:

**On [Objection 1 — e.g., Budget]:**  
You mentioned $4,800 feels high for a pilot. I hear that. A few options:
- **Reduced pilot:** 25 units instead of 50 (drops to $2,800, same timeline)
- **Revenue share:** If you're willing to give feedback and refer peers, we can negotiate the pilot rate
- **Phased approach:** Start with 25 units, prove ROI, expand to 50 in month 2

**On [Objection 2 — e.g., Custom Feature X]:**  
You asked about [specific feature]. Honestly, we're not there yet, but it's on the roadmap. That said, here's what we *can* do:
- [Workaround solution or timeline for feature]
- Connect you with [similar customer] who solved this a different way

**What doesn't change:**
- 10-minute provisioning (we own that)
- 95%+ brand fidelity (automated, not manual)
- 14-day delivery (hard constraint)

**My ask:**  
Would a **reduced-scope pilot (25 units, $2,800)** starting [date] work? Or do you need to think through the [objection] more?

Either way, I'm here to find a path forward. What feels realistic?

[Your Name]

---

### Personalization Notes
- **Only address objections they actually stated**—don't over-anticipate
- Offer 2-3 concrete trade-offs, not vague promises
- Show you listened by referencing their exact concern
- Use collaborative language: "Let's figure this out together"
- This is the highest-ROI follow-up template—many deals happen here

## Discovery Call Script (Interview Guide)

**Duration:** 30-40 minutes  
**Format:** Semi-structured (follow the flow, but let conversation breathe)  
**Recording:** Mention that you're taking notes; ask permission to record (optional but recommended)

### Opening (2 min)

*"Thanks for making time. I know you're busy. For the next 30-40 minutes, I just want to understand your world—no pitch, no pressure. I'll share a quick demo of what we do, but mostly I'm here to listen. Sound good?"*

### Part 1: Current State (10 min)

**Lead questions:**
1. "Walk me through your onboarding-kit process today. Where does it start and end?"
2. "How long does the whole thing take from brief to delivery?"
3. "Who's involved? (You, vendors, other teams, etc.)"
4. "What frustrates you most about the current way?"
5. "What does success look like? What would you optimize for first—speed, cost, quality, simplicity?"

**Listening cues:**
- Take notes on: timeline, pain points, decision-makers, budget signals
- Ask "why?" once or twice to dig deeper
- Don't defend or pitch yet

### Part 2: Demo & Reaction (10 min)

*"Let me show you how we approach this differently. [Share screen]"*

**Walk through:**
- Enter a test domain (e.g., ramp.com)
- Show live brand extraction (colors, logos, fonts)
- Show generated storefront preview
- Highlight mockup generation
- "This took us 8 minutes. Your current process takes ___."

**Gauge reaction:**
- "What do you think?"
- "How does that compare to what you're doing now?"
- "What would make this even better for you?"

### Part 3: Price & Feature Fit (10-15 min)

**Anchor the price:**
"If we could deliver a 50-unit onboarding kit with your exact brand colors and logo in 14 days for $4,800—roughly $96 per unit—would that be compelling compared to what you're spending now?"

**Listen for:**
- Yes / Strong yes → Move to pilot (Template 3)
- Maybe / Conditional → Probe on what would tip it (Template 4)
- No / Too expensive → Understand constraint, offer scaled version (Template 4)

**Feature questions:**
- "What's missing from what I showed you?"
- "Is there a feature you need that would make this a no-brainer?"
- "How does this compare to [SwagUp / Stadium / Printful / etc.]?"

### Part 4: Pilot & Closing (5-10 min)

**Pilot offer:**
"Here's what I'm thinking: A 30-day Brand Drop Pilot. You get 50 units delivered in 14 days. You use them with one real cohort. You give us feedback. If it works, we scale. If it doesn't, we learn and iterate. Price is $4,800 for this pilot. No long-term contract. What do you think?"

**Listen for commitment level:**
- **Verbal yes** → "Great. Here's next steps..." (send Template 5)
- **Let me think about it** → "Totally fair. When's a good time to follow up?" (Agree on timeline, send Template 3)
- **Need to loop in [stakeholder]** → "Who else should I sync with?" (Schedule group call)

**Close:**
"Thanks for the honest conversation. I'll send over next steps [today/tomorrow]. You'll have everything you need to decide. I'm excited about this—excited to help you move faster."

## Key Messaging Anchors (Reference)

| Topic | Talking Point | Why It Works |
|-------|---------------|--------------|
| **Speed** | "10 minutes to live storefront" | Concrete, measurable, big delta vs. 4-8 weeks |
| **Quality** | "95%+ brand fidelity, automated not manual" | Removes risk of QA failures |
| **Price** | "$96/unit for 50-unit kit" | Makes it feel affordable vs. $5-15K service model |
| **Timeline** | "14 days delivery SLA" | Clear promise, builds confidence |
| **Ease** | "Just give us your domain" | No heavy lift from customer |
| **Risk** | "30-day pilot, no long-term lock" | Removes objection about commitment |

## Final Tips

1. **Personalize every email.** Use their actual company name, pain points, and stakeholder titles.
2. **Send templates within 2 hours of the trigger event.** (Response = send invite. Call ends = send follow-up.)
3. **Use Calendly or similar for calls.** Reduces back-and-forth by 80%.
4. **Record calls (with permission).** Review for tone, objection handling, and pitch effectiveness.
5. **Template chaining matters.** Invitation → Confirmation → Post-Call → Pilot Formalization. Each builds on the last.
6. **Adapt, don't rigidly follow.** If they say something important, pivot. These are guides, not scripts.
