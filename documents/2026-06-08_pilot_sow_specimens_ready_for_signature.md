---

### I. PARTIES

**SERVICE PROVIDER:** Branded Fit, Inc. ("Provider")  
**CLIENT:** [INSERT COMPANY NAME]  
**AUTHORIZED REPRESENTATIVE:** [INSERT CONTACT NAME], [INSERT TITLE]  
**CONTACT EMAIL:** [INSERT EMAIL ADDRESS]

---

### II. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Branded Fit will design, provision, and optimize an autonomous, AI-driven merchandise storefront for [INSERT COMPANY NAME], powered by real-time brand extraction (Brandfetch), production integration (Printify), and e-commerce hosting (Shopify). This pilot validates:

- **Brand extraction accuracy** via Brandfetch API (target: ≥95%)
- **Speed-to-market** via Printify + Shopify provisioning (target: <10 minutes)
- **Storefront visual fidelity** via NPS-based rating (target: ≥4/5)
- **Fulfillment delivery** of 50-unit onboarding kit (target: ≤14 days)
- **Customer satisfaction** via NPS survey (target: >7 NPS score)

If 2 or more success metrics are met AND Client reports >7 NPS, Client may expand to Branded Fit's annual Growth contract at $24,000 (auto-expansion clause).

---

### III. SCOPE OF WORK

**Phase 1: Domain Intake & Brand Extraction (Days 1-2)**
- Receive [INSERT COMPANY NAME] domain and brand guidelines
- Run Brandfetch API extraction on company domain, website, logo library
- Extract primary brand colors (hex), logo files, brand voice keywords
- Deliver extracted brand charter to Client for review & approval
- **Deliverable:** Brand Charter Document (JSON + visual kit)

**Phase 2: Storefront Design & Provisioning (Days 3-5)**
- Design autonomous storefront template using extracted brand colors, logo, messaging
- Create mockups for 5-10 hero products
- Integrate with Printify's on-demand production API
- Configure Stripe checkout for seamless payment flow
- Set up Shopify hosting + domain routing
- Deliver storefront preview link to Client for feedback
- **Deliverable:** Live Storefront Preview + Stripe Checkout Test Link

**Phase 3: 50-Unit Onboarding Kit Fulfillment (Days 5-14)**
- Client selects 5 hero products and quantities for physical onboarding kit
- Provider coordinates with Printify for on-demand production
- Arrange fulfillment to Client office (domestic shipping included)
- Track delivery with Printify + courier integration
- **Deliverable:** Physical 50-Unit Onboarding Kit + Delivery Confirmation

**Phase 4: Analytics & Optimization (Days 15-30)**
- Instrument storefront with daily event tracking
- Build daily analytics dashboard (conversion funnel, time-series, event counts)
- Provide daily optimization recommendations
- Conduct weekly check-ins with Client
- **Deliverable:** Daily Analytics Dashboard + Weekly Optimization Reports

**Phase 5: Pilot Completion & Decision Support (Day 30)**
- Deliver final pilot summary report with success metrics scorecard
- Facilitate NPS survey with Client stakeholders
- Present go/no-go recommendation for annual Growth contract expansion
- **Deliverable:** Final Pilot Report + NPS Results + Expansion Decision

---

### IV. SUCCESS METRICS

Provider will consider the pilot **successful** if the following metrics are achieved by **July 10, 2026**:

| Metric | Target | Measurement Method | Weight |
|--------|--------|-------------------|--------|
| **Brand Extraction Accuracy** | ≥95% | Brandfetch API outputs validated against brand guidelines | 33% |
| **Provisioning Time** | <10 minutes | Time from domain submission to live storefront | 33% |
| **Storefront Visual Fidelity** | ≥4/5 (NPS-style) | Client stakeholder rating | 33% |
| **Fulfillment Delivery** | ≤14 days | Physical onboarding kit delivery time | Pass/Fail |
| **Customer Satisfaction (NPS)** | >7 NPS | Post-pilot survey | Expansion qualifier |

**Conversion Criteria:** If 2+ primary metrics are met AND NPS >7, Client may expand to annual Growth contract at $24,000/year.

**Payment Schedule:**

- Invoice issued within 2 business days

- Shopify hosting (30-day trial + configuration)
- Stripe checkout integration

**What's NOT Included:**

**Refund Policy:** Non-refundable. However, if Provider fails to deliver any of the five core deliverables by scheduled dates, Client may request a 50% refund for each missed deliverable.

**1. Data & Brand Assets**

**2. Confidentiality**
Both parties agree not to disclose financial terms, pilot results, or technical architecture to third parties without written consent. Exception: Provider may list Client as pilot customer in case studies with prior approval.

**3. Limitation of Liability**
Provider's total liability for any claim related to this SOW shall not exceed the pilot fee ($4,800). Neither party is liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages.

**4. Termination**
Either party may terminate with 5 business days' written notice. If Client terminates, no refund is owed except as noted in Refund Policy. If Provider terminates, Client is entitled to a 50% refund of remaining undelivered phases.

**5. Change Orders**
Scope changes beyond the standard 5-phase plan require a signed change order. No changes are binding without written amendment.

**6. Growth Contract Transition**
If conversion criteria are met by July 10, 2026, Provider will present a 12-month Growth contract at $24,000/year. Client has 5 business days to decide on expansion.

**7. Dispute Resolution**
Any dispute shall be resolved through good-faith negotiation within 10 business days. If unresolved, either party may pursue mediation or arbitration per applicable law. All claims must be brought within 1 year of pilot completion.
