**Status:** Strategic Research & Validation Framework

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Five customers have completed the Brand Drop Pilot ($4,800 each) and are now active users of Branded Fit's storefront generation product. This cohort represents the highest-signal validation opportunity we have: real usage, real commitment, and real feedback.

This document establishes a **structured discovery call framework** to validate or refute six critical assumptions that determine whether:
1. **$24K annual Growth tier pricing holds** (willingness-to-pay validation)
2. **Y1 product roadmap is right** (use-case expansion signals)
3. **Pilot-to-contract conversion is achievable** (expansion revenue path)

The framework is designed for **immediate execution** with 45-minute calls, using a **fixed question script** to ensure comparability across all 5 customers. Each call will capture:
- **Brand-fidelity NPS** (1–10 scale, target ≥8)
- **Provisioning-speed perception** (minutes from domain → mockup, target ≤10 min)
- **$24K annual WTP** (yes/no/maybe classification)
- **Warm-intro source** (referral path attribution)
- **Expansion use-cases** (beyond onboarding kits)
- **Storefront impact on brand perception** (NPS shift, employee feedback)

Results will feed directly into **Step 23 (Dogs Will Eat Dog Food)** founder decision memo, which determines go/no-go on pricing and product strategy.

## SECTION 1: DISCOVERY CALL FRAMEWORK

### 1.1 Call Structure & Timing

**Total Duration:** 45 minutes  
**Format:** Video call (Zoom/Google Meet), recorded with explicit consent  
**Attendees:** 
- Branded Fit side: Founder (primary) + researcher (note-taker)
- Customer side: Primary decision-maker (People Ops, HR, CMO, or Exec Sponsor)

**Timing Breakdown:**
- **Opening (0–3 min):** Rapport, context-setting, recording consent
- **Discovery (3–18 min):** Open-ended discovery (Questions 1–6) — 70% listening, 30% talking
- **Assumption Validation (18–38 min):** Structured WTP ladder (Question 3 drilling), use-case exploration (Question 5 drilling)
- **Close (38–45 min):** Reference willingness, next steps, thank you

### 1.2 Pre-Call Preparation Checklist

**Researcher (48 hours before call):**
- [ ] Confirm call link + calendar invite sent (confirm receipt)
- [ ] Review customer's pilot history: domain submitted, mockup approved, order date
- [ ] Identify any public brand guidelines or design references (company website, LinkedIn brand updates)
- [ ] Prepare printed or digital scorecard template for real-time note-taking
- [ ] Test Zoom recording, speaker, and internet connectivity
- [ ] Prepare 2–3 personalized opening remarks about customer's brand or industry

**Founder:**
- [ ] Review customer's storefront output (if publicly viewable)
- [ ] Identify 2 specific positive outcomes or mockup details to reference
- [ ] Prepare 30-second origin story ("We built Branded Fit because...")
- [ ] Mentally rehearse top 3 objection counters (SwagUp comparison, brand-fidelity confidence, pricing justification)
- [ ] Set up phone on silent; disable Slack/email notifications

**Logistics:**
- [ ] Backup Zoom link prepared in case technical issue
- [ ] Google Drive folder created for call notes + recording link
- [ ] DocuSign template ready if reference agreement requested

### 1.3 Tone & Approach

**Consultant, not Salesperson:**
- Frame as "We're learning from our best customers about what's working and what we should build next"
- **Avoid:** pitch, pressure, selling annual plan during call
- **Embrace:** curiosity, active listening, genuine interest in their swag workflow

**Peer-to-Peer, Founder Energy:**
- Speak as founder, not account manager
- Reference your own uncertainty on pricing ("We're testing $24K—what feels right to you?")
- Admit product limitations honestly ("We know mockups take 10 min—that's a known friction point")

**70/30 Listen/Talk Ratio:**
- Ask question, then **pause for 30+ seconds** to let customer think
- Avoid filling silence with talking
- Use "Tell me more about that..." instead of suggesting answers

## SECTION 2: FIXED QUESTION SCRIPT

### Question 1: Brand Fidelity NPS (Target: ≥8/10)
**Objective:** Validate that generated storefronts match brand identity accurately  
**Assumption Threshold:** ≥8/10 average across 5 customers = CONFIRM; <7/10 = REFUTE; 7–7.9 = GRAY

**Script:**
> "Let's talk about the mockup we generated for you. On a scale of 1 to 10, where 1 is 'completely missed our brand' and 10 is 'looks like we designed it ourselves'—how accurately did the storefront match your actual brand identity?"

**Follow-Up Questions (if needed):**
- "What specific elements matched well? What felt off?"
- "If you showed this to your CEO/CMO, what would be their first impression?"
- "Did the color palette, typography, or layout surprise you in any way?"

**Note-Taker:**
- Record verbatim score (1–10)
- Capture 2–3 specific details they mention (e.g., "loved the blue palette match" or "fonts felt too corporate")
- Flag any mention of brand guideline adherence issues

**Evidence Label:** Brand-Fidelity NPS for [Customer Name]

---

### Question 2: Provisioning Speed Perception (Target: ≤10 minutes)
**Objective:** Validate that mockup turnaround time meets internal perception targets  
**Assumption Threshold:** ≤10 min average = CONFIRM; >15 min = REFUTE; 10–15 min = GRAY

**Script:**
> "From the moment you submitted your domain to seeing the first mockup, how long do you think it took? Roughly in minutes."

**Follow-Up Questions (if needed):**
- "Did that feel fast or slow for what you were getting?"
- "How would you rank this vs. other vendors (SwagUp, custom design agency, internal design team)?"
- "Would 5 minutes feel notably different than 10 minutes for your use case?"

**Note-Taker:**
- Record estimated time (even if approximate; "I'd guess 5–8 minutes")
- Note whether customer perceived speed positively, neutrally, or negatively
- Flag any perception mismatch ("felt instant, but I bet it was 15 minutes behind scenes")

**Evidence Label:** Provisioning Speed Perception for [Customer Name]

---

### Question 3: Willingness-to-Pay ($24K Annual Growth Tier)
**Objective:** Validate that $24K annual pricing point is justified and achievable for pilot-to-contract conversion  
**Assumption Threshold:** ≥4/5 "yes" answers = CONFIRM; ≤2/5 = REFUTE; 3/5 = GRAY

**Script (Van Westendorp Ladder):**
> "Let's talk about pricing for the annual plan. We're testing a Growth tier at $24,000/year, which includes 18,000 swag credits and access to our Brand DNA platform for custom matching.

**Tier A ($24K/year anchor):**
> Would you sign a contract for that annual Growth tier, yes or no?"

- **If YES:** "That's great! What would need to change for you to say no?"
  - *→ Capture deal-breaker or confidence threshold*
- **If NO or HESITANT:** "What price would feel more comfortable for you?"
  - *→ Listen for alternate price point or value request*

**Tier B (If they hesitate at $24K, test down):**
> "What if we did $18K/year with the same credits and platform access?"

**Tier C (If $18K still no):**
> "What's the price point at which you'd seriously consider us vs. staying with [current vendor]?"

**Note-Taker:**
- Record yes/no/hesitant on $24K
- If yes: capture confidence level (1–5, where 5 is "I'll sign today")
- If no: capture stated objection (price too high, not enough credits, missing feature X)
- Record alternate price points mentioned
- Capture any reference to budget, approval process, or internal ROI hurdle rate

**Evidence Label:** WTP Validation for [Customer Name] — $24K/year Growth Tier

### Question 4: Warm-Intro Conversion (Referral Source Tracking)
**Objective:** Understand conversion path and identify warm-path strength  
**Evidence Goal:** Track attribution for future GTM efficiency

**Script:**
> "I'd love to understand how you found out about Branded Fit. Was it a recommendation, an article, a cold email, or something else?"

**Follow-Up Questions:**
- "If it was a recommendation, who introduced you and what did they say?"
- "What convinced you to take the first step (demo, pilot sign-up, etc.)?"
- "Would you be comfortable being a reference customer and recommending us to peers?"

**Note-Taker:**
- Capture referral source (warm intro, cold email, company blog, LinkedIn, conference, etc.)
- If warm intro: capture referrer name/company
- Record conversion friction (was it easy to move from awareness to pilot?)
- Capture reference willingness: Yes / No / Maybe

**Evidence Label:** Warm-Intro Conversion for [Customer Name]

### Question 5: Expansion Use-Cases Beyond Onboarding Kits (Product Roadmap Signal)
**Objective:** Validate that Branded Fit has multi-use-case value beyond onboarding swag  
**Evidence Goal:** Inform Y1 product roadmap prioritization

**Script:**
> "We know the Brand Drop Pilot is focused on onboarding swag—new hire t-shirts, hoodies, etc. But I'm curious: beyond onboarding kits, what other use cases do you see for Branded Fit in your company? Where else might custom branded swag or a branded storefront add value?"

**Listen For:**
- **Team merchandise** (engineering swag, marketing merch)
- **Customer appreciation** (client gifts, customer onboarding)
- **Internal culture** (company events, hackathons, all-hands swag)
- **Employee retention/engagement** (milestone gifts, wellness kits)
- **Partner/channel programs** (partner onboarding, reseller kits)
- **Seasonal campaigns** (holiday gifts, quarterly merch drops)

**Follow-Up Questions:**
- "Of those use cases, which one would you prioritize first?"
- "Would the platform features you see now support that use case, or would you need [custom mockups/dynamic inventory/etc.]?"
- "What's stopping you from using Branded Fit for those use cases today?"

**Note-Taker:**
- List all use cases mentioned (even speculative ones)
- Rank by customer priority (which would they fund/invest in first?)
- Capture feature gaps ("We'd need dynamic mockup approval," "Monthly swag drops would help")
- Identify expansion revenue potential ("Could easily spend $2K/month on ongoing team merch")

**Evidence Label:** Expansion Use-Cases for [Customer Name]

### Question 6: Storefront Impact on Brand Perception & Onboarding Experience
**Objective:** Validate that branded storefronts improve employee brand perception and onboarding NPS  
**Evidence Goal:** Validate long-term retention and expansion conversion drivers

**Script:**
> "Since you launched the Branded Fit storefront for your new hires, have you noticed any change in how employees perceive your brand? Or has it impacted the onboarding experience in any way?"

**Listen For:**
- **Brand perception shift** ("New hires now see us as a design-forward company")
- **Onboarding delight factor** ("They get better swag on day 1, which feels premium")
- **Employee retention signal** ("People talk about how good the branded gear is")
- **Quantified NPS impact** ("Our onboarding NPS went from X to Y")
- **Hiring narrative** ("This is now part of how we pitch our employer brand")

**Follow-Up Questions:**
- "Can you share any feedback from new hires about the swag or storefront experience?"
- "Have you measured any impact on retention or onboarding satisfaction scores?"
- "Would you want to show this to your CEO/CFO as a win?"

**Note-Taker:**
- Capture qualitative feedback (2–3 verbatim quotes from new hires or exec feedback)
- Record any quantified metric (NPS change, retention rate, feedback score)
- Flag any unexpected insights ("Used it for team building, not just onboarding")
- Assess strategic value perception ("This is now core to our employer brand")

**Evidence Label:** Storefront Impact for [Customer Name]

## SECTION 6: ASSUMPTION VALIDATION THRESHOLDS & DECISION RULES

### A1: Brand-Fidelity NPS ≥8/10
- **Success Threshold:** ≥4/5 customers rate ≥8 (avg ≥8.0)
- **Failure Threshold:** ≤2/5 customers rate ≥8 (avg ≤7.0)
- **Gray Zone:** 3/5 customers rate ≥8 (avg 7.0–7.9)
- **Remediation (if GRAY/REFUTE):** Run brand-fidelity design review with customer to identify Brandfetch extraction gaps; prioritize design refinement in Q3 roadmap

### A2: Provisioning Speed ≤10 minutes
- **Success Threshold:** ≥4/5 customers perceive turnaround ≤10 min (avg ≤10 min)
- **Failure Threshold:** ≤2/5 customers perceive ≤10 min (avg >12 min)
- **Gray Zone:** 3/5 perceive ≤10 min (avg 10–12 min)
- **Remediation (if GRAY/REFUTE):** Measure actual provisioning speed end-to-end; identify bottlenecks (Brandfetch extraction time, Printify API lag, Shopify sync). Target <6 min for Q3.

### A3: $24K Annual WTP (≥4/5 Yes)
- **Success Threshold:** ≥4/5 customers say yes to $24K or hesitant-but-not-opposed
- **Failure Threshold:** ≤2/5 say yes; majority push back on price
- **Gray Zone:** 3/5 say yes; price sensitivity flagged
- **Remediation (if GRAY/REFUTE):** Test lower price point ($18K–$20K) with hesitant customers; assess value prop gap ("what would make $24K a yes?"); consider payment plan or credit-based model

### A4: Warm-Intro Conversion Rate
- **Success Threshold:** ≥3/5 customers came through warm intro (peer referral)
- **Evidence:** Referrer name, referral source, conversion time from intro → pilot
- **Remediation (if LOW):** Identify top customers + peer networks; prioritize reference customer program; build case studies for existing customers to share

### A5: Expansion Use-Cases (≥2 per customer avg)
- **Success Threshold:** Each customer identifies ≥2 use-cases beyond onboarding (avg ≥2 per customer)
- **Product Insight:** Use-cases ranked by customer priority → inform Q3 roadmap
- **Remediation (if LOW):** Conduct deeper product discovery; validate that platform pricing/mechanics support expansion use-cases; design feature roadmap around top 3 use-cases

### A6: Storefront Impact on Brand Perception & Retention
- **Success Threshold:** ≥3/5 customers report positive brand perception shift OR quantified NPS/retention lift
- **Evidence Type:** Qualitative (employee feedback, CEO perception) + Quantified (NPS delta, retention rate)
- **Remediation (if GRAY/REFUTE):** Run post-launch survey with new hire recipients to measure perception directly; ask about employer brand impact; measure onboarding NPS

## SECTION 8: OBJECTION HANDLING & COUNTERS

**During calls, customer may raise objections. Use these counters:**

### Objection 1: "Your mockups look generic / don't feel premium enough"
**Counter:**
- "That's valuable feedback. Brand fidelity depends on how well we extract your brand DNA from Brandfetch. Are there specific design elements (color, font, layout) that felt off?"
- "We're actually improving the Brandfetch integration in Q3 to handle more complex brand guidelines. What's the top 1–2 design gaps we should fix?"
- *→ Reframe as product improvement signal, not a pricing objection*

### Objection 2: "Provisioning is slow—we need sub-5-minute turnaround"
**Counter:**
- "What's the business context? Is it for onboarding swag where 10 min is acceptable, or use-cases where you need near-real-time (like event merchandise)?"
- "Our current architecture is optimized for accuracy over speed. For real-time use cases, we're exploring on-demand mockup approvals. What would $18K/year with faster turnaround look like to you?"
- *→ Separate use-case and validate value trade-offs*

### Objection 3: "$24K is too expensive—we can get cheaper swag elsewhere"
**Counter:**
- "Totally fair. Our value prop isn't cheapest swag—it's brand-fidelity + on-demand fulfillment + platform. If we locked in price at $18K, would the ROI math work for you?"
- "Or alternatively: What's your annual swag budget today? How much of that could we capture if we cut price to $X?"
- *→ Understand true budget constraint vs. pricing objection; test down systematically*

### Objection 4: "We don't see the need for annual commitment—one-off campaigns are fine"
**Counter:**
- "I hear that. For now, your needs might be onboarding-only. But here's what we're seeing with peers: team merchandise (engineering swag, quarterly drops, events) becomes a growth lever once they see how easy it is. Would you be open to revisiting annual if those use-cases materialized?"
- "In the meantime, would a 3-month pilot at lower price make sense to prove ROI before annual commitment?"
- *→ Validate expansion path; offer interim step*

### Objection 5: "Our internal design team can do this cheaper"
**Counter:**
- "Absolutely—for high-polish, bespoke designs, internal design is great. Our value is speed + brand consistency + on-demand (no minimum order sizes). Where's your internal team bottlenecked today? Is it approval cycles? Asset management? Vendor coordination?"
- "Here's what separates us: You approve once, we auto-apply brand DNA to any future design. That's where you save 40+ hours/year vs. manual design."
- *→ Reposition as scalability and velocity, not replacing their designer*

## SECTION 11: RISK MITIGATION & CONTINGENCIES

### Risk 1: Customer Declines or No-Shows
**Mitigation:**
- Confirm attendance 24 hours before call
- Identify backup contact at each customer (in case primary attendee unavailable)
- Reschedule within 48 hours if needed
- Offer phone call option if video tech fails

### Risk 2: Recording Fails or Permission Not Obtained
**Mitigation:**
- Have researcher transcribe key quotes during call (backup to recording)
- Confirm consent at start of call, captured in scorecard
- Send follow-up email requesting written feedback if recording consent declined

### Risk 3: Customer Becomes Sales Prospect Instead of Research
**Mitigation:**
- Frame as research call, not sales call
- Avoid closing language ("So, shall we sign a contract?")
- If customer signals readiness: "I'm glad you're interested. Let me share results with our team, and we'll loop back with a proposal if this validates our roadmap."
- Keep sales team in loop; no surprises after calls

### Risk 4: Founder Unable to Attend One Call
**Mitigation:**
- Founder pre-records 2-minute intro + question script for researcher to play
- Researcher leads call with founder as "second chair" (joins second half if possible)
- Product lead joins as substitute (must review script beforehand)

### Risk 5: Assumptions Show Mixed/Refuted Results
**Mitigation:**
- Have contingency messaging ready (e.g., "We're pivoting to value-based pricing" or "We're extending free trials")
- Schedule follow-up deep-dive calls with specific customers to diagnose (e.g., "You rated brand fidelity 6/10—what are the 2 design changes that would move you to 8?")
- Flag product gaps early; alert engineering to Q3 roadmap shifts
