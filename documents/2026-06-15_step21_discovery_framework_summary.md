## WHAT THIS FRAMEWORK SOLVES

This is a **complete, end-to-end discovery call execution framework** for validating 3 critical gray assumptions blocking the Step 21 → 22 → 24 founder decision memo:

1. **Assumption A:** $24K/year Growth tier is acceptable willingness-to-pay (WTP) for People Ops decision-makers
2. **Assumption B:** VP People / People Ops Directors have $25K signing authority without CFO escalation  
3. **Assumption C:** Series B-D company procurement cycles are realistically 14-17 days

If ≥3/3 assumptions are **CONFIRMED**, we have HIGH confidence to launch Growth tier pricing and execute sales motion.  
If 2/3 are **CONFIRMED** + 1/3 **GRAY**, we have MEDIUM confidence but need tactical adjustments.  
If ≤1/3 are **CONFIRMED**, we have LOW confidence and need to PAUSE/pivot the GTM strategy.

## HOW TO RUN ONE CALL (SIMPLIFIED)

### **Day Before**
1. Review execution plan sections: "Discovery Call Structure" + "Demo Script"
2. Memorize the 6 phases and key questions
3. Print cheat sheet
4. Prep screen share (Command Console ready)
5. Send confirmation email to prospect

### **During Call (45 min)**
1. **Opening (5 min):** Build rapport, confirm time, mention warm intro
2. **Discovery (15 min):** Ask 5 questions about current process, pain, brand, order size, success metric
3. **Demo (15 min):** Input their domain, show extracted brand, mockups, live storefront; ask how it looks
4. **Ranking (7 min):** Forced-rank Speed vs. Brand Fidelity vs. No MOQs; ask why #1 matters
5. **Pricing (13 min):** Present $24K Growth tier; ask if acceptable; probe authority; ask procurement cycle
6. **Close (5 min):** Offer $4,800 Brand Drop Pilot; get YES/MAYBE/NO; set expectations

### **After Call (2 hours)**
1. Fill in data tracker sheet (from execution plan)
2. Score each assumption: CONFIRM / GRAY / REFUTE
3. Send thank-you email with SOW (if YES/MAYBE)
4. Update master call summary table

### **After All 5 Calls**
1. Tally CONFIRM/GRAY/REFUTE for each assumption
2. Fill in results template sections (scorecard, synthesis, memo)
3. Schedule founder debrief
4. Present GO/PIVOT/PAUSE recommendation with evidence

## CRITICAL SUCCESS FACTORS

1. **Capture Verbatim Quotes** — Don't paraphrase. Write down exactly what they said about pricing, authority, cycle. These become evidence in founder memo.

2. **Score Conservatively** — If you're uncertain whether response is CONFIRM or GRAY, mark GRAY. Better to err conservative on validation thresholds.

3. **Ask Follow-ups** — If you get a hedged answer (e.g., "maybe $24K"), ask clarifying question: "Is that a yes in principle pending X, or are you still evaluating?" Push for clarity.

4. **Measure Demo Reaction in Real Time** — Watch body language, enthusiasm level when they see mockups. Rate wow-factor immediately (1-5), not after call.

5. **Get Contact Information** — Confirm email, phone, decision timeline. If MAYBE, confirm next follow-up date before call ends.

6. **Complete Post-Call Checklist** — Don't wait. Fill tracker, score assumptions, send thank-you email within 2 hours while details are fresh.

7. **Aggregate Early** — After 3 calls, see if pattern is emerging (e.g., all 3 say $24K is too expensive). Adjust messaging for remaining 2 calls if needed.

## WHAT TO DO IF...

### **...Assumptions A/B/C are all CONFIRMED by call 2?**
→ No need to adjust messaging. Keep running remaining calls to confirm trend. Prep for GO decision.

### **...Assumption A (WTP) is REFUTED by call 1?**
→ **Adjust for calls 2-5:** Shift messaging to ROI/payback ("Often breaks even in 6 months"). Test lower anchor ($18K) on call 2 to see if reception improves.

### **...Assumption B (Authority) is REFUTED by all 3 calls so far?**
→ **Pivot Sales Motion:** CFO engagement is required. Develop CFO approval letter, ROI summary. Extend cycle to 30 days.

### **...Assumption C (Cycle) is REFUTED (all prospects say 30+ days)?**
→ **Extend Model:** Sales cycle is 4+ weeks, not 2-3. Adjust founder expectations. Develop "fast-track" option (10-day cycle) with pre-signed MSA.

## FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

**Q: Do I need to conduct all 5 calls before scoring?**  
A: No. You can score after each call and track emerging patterns. But wait until all 5 are complete before finalizing GO/PIVOT/PAUSE recommendation.

**Q: What if a prospect asks for a discount from $24K?**  
A: This is a GRAY response, not REFUTE. They're interested but price-sensitive. Note the objection, but this doesn't mean $24K is unacceptable. If ≥3/5 accept $24K without objection, assumption is still CONFIRMED.

**Q: What if a prospect says they can't decide without their CEO?**  
A: Mark authority as LIMITED. This is a GRAY or REFUTE on Assumption B (we need VP-level solo authority, not CEO escalation).

**Q: What if I only get 3-4 calls scheduled before June 22?**  
A: Conduct what you can. Results from 3-4 calls are still valid; just note sample size constraint in memo (lower confidence level).

**Q: Should I record the calls?**  
A: Yes, if possible. Always ask: "Is it okay if I record this for my notes?" Recordings help you capture exact quotes later.

**Q: What if the demo has technical issues during a call?**  
A: Move on gracefully. Say: "Sorry about the tech glitch. I have mockups I can share via email instead. The key thing: we're pulling your real brand data and turning it into live storefronts in minutes." Reschedule full demo for follow-up if needed.
