## CRITICAL ASSUMPTIONS BEING TESTED

### **Assumption A: $24K Annual WTP at Growth Tier**

**Hypothesis:** People Ops managers at Series B-D companies are willing to pay $24K/year for the Growth tier (annual prepaid Swag Credits + Brand DNA SaaS).

**Validation Threshold:** ≥4/5 prospects indicate $24K is acceptable (no objections on price being "too high" or "out of budget").

**Call Probe:** 
> "We're positioning a Growth tier at $24K per year with annual prepaid Swag Credits and our Brand DNA SaaS dashboard. Walk me through your thinking—is that within a range you'd seriously consider?"

**Success Signals:**
- Prospect says: "That's reasonable" / "In the ballpark" / "We spend more on X already"
- No price objection during demo
- Prospect asks about payment terms (not cost justification)
- Prospect mentions budget allocation process (not budget constraint)

**Refute Signals:**
- "That's too expensive"
- "We'd need to go much lower"
- "Budget-wise, we're capped at $10K"
- Silence or hesitation on pricing

---

### **Assumption B: $25K Signing Authority**

**Hypothesis:** VP People / People Ops Director has unilateral signing authority for $25K spend (or close to it), meaning we can close deals directly without CFO involvement.

**Validation Threshold:** ≥3/5 prospects confirm they have (or can get) $25K authority without CFO escalation.

**Call Probe:**
> "If this is something you wanted to move forward with, what's your approval process? Do you have signing authority for, say, a $24K annual contract, or would you need CFO sign-off?"

**Success Signals:**
- "I can approve up to $50K on my own"
- "I'm the P&L owner for People operations"
- "My VP sign-off is all we need"
- Prospect is clearly confident in their authority

**Gray Signals:**
- "My CFO usually reviews anything over $20K, but we can probably get it approved"
- "It depends on the contract terms"
- "I'd loop in finance, but I drive the decision"

**Refute Signals:**
- "Everything over $5K needs CFO approval"
- "I'm not a budget decision-maker"
- "This would need to go through procurement"

---

### **Assumption C: 14-17 Day Procurement Cycle**

**Hypothesis:** Series B-D companies can move from discovery call to contract signature in 14-17 days (our current model).

**Validation Threshold:** ≥3/5 prospects indicate their actual cycle is within 14-20 days (allowing for minor variance).

**Call Probe:**
> "If you were excited about moving forward, what does your vendor onboarding timeline look like? From initial agreement to signed contract, how many days do you typically need?"

**Success Signals:**
- "We can move fast—probably 1-2 weeks"
- "If it's SaaS, usually 10-14 days"
- "Finance turnaround is 5 days max"
- "We use a standard MSA, so signature is quick"

**Gray Signals:**
- "Normally 3-4 weeks, but could be faster if prioritized"
- "Depends on legal review, could be 2-3 weeks"
- "We try to keep it under 20 days"

**Refute Signals:**
- "Our standard procurement is 6+ weeks"
- "Legal review alone takes a month"
- "We move slowly on new vendors"

## DISCOVERY CALL STRUCTURE (45–60 MIN)

### **Phase 1: Opening & Rapport (0-5 min) | 5 min**

**Objective:** Build trust, establish credibility, confirm call parameters.

**Script:**
> "Thanks so much for taking time today. I'm [Your Name], I founded Branded Fit—we're building AI-powered brand-controlled merchandise storefronts for People ops teams. You were referred by [warm intro source], and I've been excited to chat.
>
> Before we dive in, I want to confirm—do you have about 45 minutes? And is this a good time, or should we reschedule?
>
> Quick agenda: I'll ask about your current swag/merch process, walk through our platform live, and get your honest feedback. Does that work?"

**Validation:**
- [ ] Prospect confirms 45-min window
- [ ] Prospect is in quiet location (can hear well)
- [ ] No urgent interruptions announced

---

### **Phase 2: Discovery & Pain Mapping (5-20 min) | 15 min**

**Objective:** Understand their current process, pain points, success metrics. Map to 3 pillars: **Speed, Brand Fidelity, No MOQs**.

**Questions (Ask all 5, listen 70%, talk 30%):**

1. **Current Process:**
   > "Walk me through your current swag / merchandise process. Who owns it? How often do you do it?"
   
   *Listen for:* Budget, frequency, pain points, stakeholders.

2. **Pain Points:**
   > "What's the biggest headache in that process? What takes the most time or causes the most back-and-forth?"
   
   *Listen for:* Speed issues, brand compliance, vendor pain, cost.

3. **Brand Alignment:**
   > "How important is it that swag actually reflects your brand? Do you have brand guidelines, and do vendors respect them?"
   
   *Listen for:* Brand fidelity maturity, current compliance level, designer involvement.

4. **MOQ / Ordering:**
   > "What's your typical order size? Do you ever run into minimum order quantities that feel too high?"
   
   *Listen for:* Scale, frequency, MOQ sensitivity.

5. **Success Metric:**
   > "If we could improve one thing about your current process, what would it be? How would you measure success?"
   
   *Listen for:* Priority ranking (speed vs. fidelity vs. flexibility), ROI lens.

**Data to Capture:**
- [ ] Current vendor(s)
- [ ] Budget range
- [ ] Frequency (quarterly, annual, ad-hoc)
- [ ] Key pain (cite verbatim)
- [ ] Brand importance level (1-5)
- [ ] Typical order size
- [ ] Success metric

---

### **Phase 3: Product Demo & Reaction Measurement (20-35 min) | 15 min**

**Objective:** Walk through Command Console live. Measure wow-factor, brand-fidelity satisfaction, time-to-live-store perception.

**Demo Script:**

> "Let me show you how Branded Fit works. I'm going to input [Prospect's Company Domain] into our Command Console and show you what happens in real time."

**Step 1: Domain Input (1 min)**
- Navigate to branded-fit.vercel.app/command-console
- Ask prospect for company domain
- Input domain, hit submit
- Say: "Behind the scenes, we're extracting your brand—colors, logos, typography—all automatically."

**Step 2: Brand Extraction (2 min)**
- Share screen with live brand extraction in progress
- Show extracted colors, logos
- Ask: "Does that feel like your brand? Are these the right brand colors?"
  
  *Capture:* Gut reaction (enthusiastic/neutral/critical). Note any corrections they'd make.

**Step 3: Mockup Generation (2 min)**
- Move to mockup preview
- Show 3-5 generated product mockups with brand applied
- Ask: "How do these look? Do they feel on-brand?"
  
  *Capture:* Wow moment (yes/maybe/no), brand fidelity rating (1-5), specific feedback.

**Step 4: Storefront Live Preview (2 min)**
- Show live storefront with products
- Explain: "This would be live in minutes. Your team could start taking orders today."
- Ask: "What's your gut reaction to seeing your merch go live this fast?"
  
  *Capture:* Speed perception (impressed/expected/skeptical), time-to-live assessment.

**Step 5: Close Demo (6 min)**
- Return to Q&A: "Any questions on what you just saw?"
- Listen to objections / clarifications
- Address brand-fidelity concerns
- Reinforce 3 pillars: speed, fidelity, no MOQs

**Wow-Factor Scoring:**
- **5 (Wow):** Prospect says "That's amazing" / "I didn't know this was possible" / "This changes everything"
- **4 (Impressed):** Positive reaction, asks follow-up questions, sees clear value
- **3 (Neutral):** "That works" / Nods but no strong reaction
- **2 (Skeptical):** Questions quality / "How accurate is the brand extraction?"
- **1 (Dismissive):** "We already do this" / No enthusiasm

**Brand Fidelity Rating (1-5 scale):**
- Ask directly: "On a scale of 1-5, how accurately did we capture your brand in these mockups?"

---

### **Phase 4: Purchasing Criteria Ranking (35-42 min) | 7 min**

**Objective:** Forced-rank top 3 purchasing criteria to validate hypothesis that **Speed > Brand Fidelity > No MOQs** matters most.

**Script:**
> "I want to understand what matters most to you in a solution like this. I'm going to give you three key benefits, and I want you to rank them 1–3 (1 = most important, 3 = least important). There's no right answer—just your honest take.
>
> The three factors are:
> 1. **Speed** — From discovery to live storefront in minutes/hours (not weeks)
> 2. **Brand Fidelity** — Mockups and storefronts that truly reflect your brand identity
> 3. **On-Demand Fulfillment with No MOQs** — No forced bulk orders; order what you need when you need it
>
> How would you rank these for your organization?"

**Capture Verbatim Rankings:**

- [ ] Rank 2 (Important): _______________
- [ ] Rank 3 (Least Important): _______________

**Follow-up Question (if needed):**

## APPENDIX: VAN WESTENDORP WTP LADDER (Optional)

If you want to get more sophisticated WTP data, use this pricing ladder approach:

> "I'm going to read you four price points. For each, tell me: too cheap (red flag), cheap (good value), expensive, or too expensive (won't buy).
>
> 1. **$12K/year** — How does that feel?
> 2. **$24K/year** — How does that feel?
> 3. **$36K/year** — How does that feel?
> 4. **$48K/year** — How does that feel?"

**Analysis:** Plot responses to find:
- **Price ceiling** (where "too expensive" exceeds 50%)
- **Optimal price** (where "expensive" and "cheap" cross)
- **Indifference point** (where "cheap" and "expensive" are equal)

This refines WTP beyond binary yes/no.
