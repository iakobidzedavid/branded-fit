# Discovery Call Outreach & Scheduling Templates

## Overview
This document contains 5 production-ready email templates designed to convert warm-outreach respondents into scheduled discovery calls. Each template follows the Email Template Production Readiness Checklist and is segmented by buyer signal and timing.

---

## Template 1: Warm Intro Follow-Up (High Intent)
**Use Case:** Responding to prospect who expressed explicit interest in pilot or pricing conversation
**Tone:** Consultative, founder energy, peer-to-peer
**Length:** 195 words

---

Thanks for your message—really appreciated. Based on what you shared about [specific pain point from their message], I think we could have a solid conversation around how Branded Fit handles [pain point translation to 3 pillars].

Here's what I'm thinking: 20-minute call where I understand your current process, what's broken, and whether our $4,800 Brand Drop Pilot makes sense for you. No pressure either way—this is just to see if we're a fit.

**A few quick questions that would help me prep:**
- How many swag drops do you run annually?
- What's your main blocker today—speed, brand accuracy, or vendor flexibility?
- Would you want to pilot something concrete in the next 60 days?

**I have these slots open:**
- Tuesday 2–4 PM PT
- Wednesday 10 AM–12 PM PT
- Thursday 3–5 PM PT

Pick one, or send me 3 times that work better for you.

One thing I'll definitely want to cover: how our 14-day fulfillment and brand-extraction accuracy compare to what you've tried before. We've seen strong signals here, but your validation matters more.

Looking forward to connecting.

[Your Name]
Founder, Branded Fit

## Template Deployment Checklist

Before sending any template:

- [ ] Customize prospect name, company, specific pain point mentioned
- [ ] Verify URLs point to https://branded-fit.vercel.app
- [ ] Confirm sender email is from branded-fit domain
- [ ] Test on mobile (Gmail, Outlook, Apple Mail)
- [ ] Verify subject line ≤50 characters
- [ ] Remove any [bracket placeholders] before sending
- [ ] Confirm no spam triggers (ALL-CAPS, excessive punctuation, "free", "limited time")
- [ ] CTA is single, clear call-to-action (schedule a time slot, not "book a call")
- [ ] Tone check: sounds like founder talking to peer, not sales rep talking to prospect

---

## Key Metrics to Track

For each outreach email sent, log in discovery_calls_tracker.md:

1. **Template Used:** (1–5 from above)
2. **Send Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
3. **Prospect Response:** (Yes/Maybe/No/No Response)
4. **Response Time:** (hours to first reply)
5. **Call Scheduled:** (Yes/No, if yes: date/time)
6. **Notes:** (any objections mentioned in response, tone, urgency signals)

---

## Success Criteria

- **Open Rate Target:** ≥40% (tracked via email provider)
- **Reply Rate Target:** ≥50% of opens
- **Call Scheduling Rate:** ≥70% of replies
- **Call Completion Rate:** ≥85% of scheduled calls (actually happen)

---

## Post-Call Logging

After each discovery call, complete the discovery_calls_tracker.md row with:
- Prospect name, title, company, call date
- Van Westendorp anchors: responses at $2K, $4.8K, $24K, $36K
- Top 3 objections
- Pilot intent: Yes/No/Maybe
- 2–3 verbatim quotes on speed, fidelity, fulfillment quality

This feeds directly into the founder decision memo (Step 6 expected outcome).
