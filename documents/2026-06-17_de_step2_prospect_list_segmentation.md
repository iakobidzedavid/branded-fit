   - Authority: Independent approval up to $100K+ (executive level)

   - (Swag often sits under Ops at early-stage companies)
   - Authority: Typically owns budget; may delegate to People team

## 4. Tier Segmentation for GTM

### Segmentation Criteria

Each tier is defined by a combination of **firmographic** (company stage, size, geography, vertical) and **behavioral** (growth rate, office model, buyer authority) signals.

---

### Tier A — High-Fit (Fast-Growing, Autonomous Buyers, Mid-Office)

**Profile:**
- Company size: 80–200 FTE (early Series B, pre-hypergrowth)
- Funding stage: Series B (primarily); early Series C
- YoY growth: 50–100%
- Office model: Hybrid (3 days/office) or remote-friendly
- Vertical: SaaS, dev tools, HR-tech, fintech
- Geography: SF Bay, NYC, Austin
- People Ops maturity: 1–2 FTE team (hands-on, founder-adjacent)

**Buyer Profile:**
- Title: People Ops Manager / Head of Employee Experience
- Reports to: VP People or CPO
- Decision authority: $18K–$50K (autonomous or 1-level escalation)
- Motivation: "We're hiring 15–25/quarter and need a way to scale onboarding + team morale with zero design overhead"

**Characteristics:**
- High swag spend velocity ($60K–$120K/year)
- Rapid onboarding kit orders (4+ per quarter)
- Familiar with automation/API workflows
- Brand-conscious (founder input on merchandise)
- Actively evaluating swag platforms (pain felt acutely)

**Win Condition:**
- Same-day onboarding kit orders
- Brand auto-extraction from domain
- Integration with BambooHR / Rippling
- Pricing: $6K–$18K/year (fits in People Ops budget)

**Est. Count in Beachhead:** ~480 companies

**Confidence:** 72% (verified via Crunchbase stage filters + hiring velocity signals)

---

### Tier B — Medium-Fit (Moderate Growth, Multi-Stakeholder Decisions, Mixed Remote)

**Profile:**
- Company size: 150–350 FTE (Series B transitioning to Series C)
- Funding stage: Series B–C (balanced)
- YoY growth: 25–50%
- Office model: Hybrid (distributed or centralized)
- Vertical: Enterprise software, fintech, data/analytics, SaaS
- Geography: Secondary hubs (Austin, LA, Seattle, distributed)
- People Ops maturity: 2–4 FTE team (formalized processes)

**Buyer Profile:**
- Title: Head of People / HR Manager / People Ops Manager
- Reports to: VP People or CPO (more structured org)
- Decision authority: $18K–$50K (often requires VP approval for >$25K)
- Motivation: "We have multiple swag vendors and no brand consistency. We need consolidated platform with reporting."

**Characteristics:**
- Moderate swag spend ($80K–$150K/year)
- Regular onboarding + team events (3–4x/year)
- Some integration experience (HRIS, ATS)
- Multi-department input on swag (finance, marketing, ops)
- Evaluating platforms methodically (longer sales cycle)

**Win Condition:**
- Consolidation of multiple vendors
- Basic reporting / spend dashboards
- HRIS integration (BambooHR, Rippling, Workday)
- Pricing: $12K–$25K/year (needs finance approval)

**Est. Count in Beachhead:** ~920 companies

**Confidence:** 68% (larger segment with more variance in buying process)

---

### Tier C — Expansion-Fit (Late-Growth, Executive-Driven, Fully Remote)

**Profile:**
- Company size: 300–500+ FTE (Series C–D)
- Funding stage: Series C–D (later stage)
- YoY growth: 15–30% (slower but sustainable)
- Office model: Fully remote, distributed, or multi-office
- Vertical: All verticals (less selective)
- Geography: Multi-region or fully distributed
- People Ops maturity: 4–8 FTE team (specialized roles)

**Buyer Profile:**
- Title: Chief People Officer / VP People / Director of People Ops
- Reports to: CEO / CPO
- Decision authority: $25K–$100K (autonomous; may involve CFO for >$50K)
- Motivation: "We need a single platform for culture, recognition, and engagement across 400+ remote employees."

**Characteristics:**
- High absolute swag spend ($120K–$300K+/year)
- High frequency (monthly recognition, quarterly events, annual all-hands)
- Complex logistics (multi-geography delivery)
- Executive-level brand stewardship
- Demanding integration requirements (Workday, BambooHR, Slack, HubSpot)

**Win Condition:**
- Enterprise-grade security / compliance
- Advanced integrations (Workday, multiple HRIS)
- Real-time reporting / spend controls
- Bulk fulfillment logistics (multi-region)
- Pricing: $24K–$50K+/year (part of broader people stack)

**Est. Count in Beachhead:** ~850 companies

**Confidence:** 65% (larger companies have more variability; overlap with non-tech sectors)

## 7. Segmentation Summary & Tier Rationale

### Why These Three Tiers?

**Tier A (21% of market)** addresses the "early adopter" use case:
- Small enough to move fast (1–2 person People team)
- Large enough to have budget ($60K–$120K/year)
- Pain is acute (hiring 15–25 people per quarter)
- Buyer authority is high (no VP approval needed for $18K)
- Conversion fastest; highest NPS and referral potential

**Tier B (41% of market)** addresses the "pragmatist" use case:
- Vendor consolidation pain (currently using 2–3 swag vendors)
- Budget approval requires VP sign-off (realistic sales cycle)
- Scale moderate (200–300 FTE); logistics manageable
- Willing to switch if ROI is clear (reporting, integrations)
- Largest segment; middle-of-market fit

**Tier C (38% of market)** addresses the "conservative" use case:
- Executive-driven evaluation (CPO/VP level)
- High absolute spend ($120K–$300K+) but long approval chains
- Demanding integration requirements (Workday, compliance, multi-region)
- Slower sales cycle but higher contract value
- Expansion revenue after product matures
