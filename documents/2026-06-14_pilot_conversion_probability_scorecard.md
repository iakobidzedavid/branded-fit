## Appendix: Scoring Methodology

### Conversion Probability Scale (1–5)

| Score | Definition | Example Interpretation |
|-------|---|---|
| **5** | **Certain to Highly Likely** | Budget confirmed, timeline clear, product-fit strong (5/5), no competitive threats → close within 8–10 weeks |
| **4** | **Highly Likely with Minor Risk** | Budget confirmed, product-fit strong (4–5/5), minor timeline slip OK (≤12 weeks), 1 risk factor present |
| **3** | **Moderate** | Budget pending OR product-fit weak (3/5) OR timeline ≥Q4, but no insurmountable blockers |
| **2** | **Unlikely** | Budget authority unclear, product-fit weak, multiple risk factors, competitive threats |
| **1** | **Very Unlikely** | Deal fundamentally broken; no clear path to commitment |

### Conversion Probability Weights

- **Budget Authority (25%):** Required gating criterion; no budget = no deal
- **Product-Fit Confidence (20%):** Feature adoption, willingness to champion internally
- **Brand Fidelity NPS (20%):** Satisfaction proxy; ≥7 = viable, ≥8.5 = strong
- **Timeline to Annual (15%):** ≤Q4 2026 preferred; Q4 is acceptable stretch
- **Competitive Positioning (10%):** Active competing evaluations = risk multiplier
- **Churn Risk (10%):** Dissatisfaction signals, budget volatility, timeline slippage

### Expansion Potential Scoring

- **Scale Tier Readiness:** Explicit interest → $60K annual commitment (2.5x base)
- **Adjacent Use Cases:** 2+ identified, each >$5K/year → expansion flag
- **Repeat Order Appetite:** Stated willingness to purchase annually or multi-quarter
