## 45-MINUTE CALL STRUCTURE

| **Phase** | **Time** | **Your Job** | **Key Questions** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Opening** | 0-5 min | Build rapport, confirm time | "Do you have 45 min?" / Mention warm intro |
| **2. Discovery** | 5-20 min | Map pain → 3 pillars (Speed, Fidelity, No MOQ) | "Walk me through current process" / "What's painful?" / "How important is brand?" / "Typical order size?" / "What would improve it?" |
| **3. Demo** | 20-35 min | Show live Command Console, measure wow-factor | Input domain → Extract brand → Generate mockups → Show live storefront. Ask: "How does that look?" / "Realistic brand?" |
| **4. Ranking** | 35-42 min | Force-rank 3 criteria (Speed vs. Fidelity vs. No MOQ) | "Rank these 1-3: Speed, Brand Fidelity, On-Demand Fulfillment" |
| **5. Pricing** | 42-55 min | Present $24K Growth tier, probe authority & cycle | "Is $24K/yr acceptable?" / "Who approves this?" / "How long procurement?" |
| **6. Close** | 55-60 min | Offer $4,800 Brand Drop Pilot | "Want to run a pilot?" / "What would change your mind?" |

### **The 3 Assumptions (Verbatim Quotes Required)**

**ASSUMPTION A — $24K WTP:**
- Ask: "Is $24K/yr in a range you'd seriously consider?"
- **Write down their EXACT words** (not your interpretation)
- Look for: Price objection? Budget constraint? Acceptance?
- **Score:** CONFIRM (yes/range) / GRAY (asks about terms) / REFUTE (too expensive)

**ASSUMPTION B — $25K Authority:**
- Ask: "Do you have authority for $24K, or CFO sign-off needed?"
- **Write down their EXACT words**
- Look for: Confidence level? Escalation required?
- **Score:** CONFIRM (full authority) / GRAY (partial/maybe CFO) / REFUTE (CFO needed)

**ASSUMPTION C — 14-17 Day Cycle:**
- Ask: "From agreement to signature, how many days?"
- **Write down specific number + bottleneck**
- Look for: Realism? Legal delays? Finance approval?
- **Score:** CONFIRM (≤20 days) / GRAY (18-25 days) / REFUTE (>25 days)

- [ ] **YES** — Ready to sign Brand Drop Pilot SOW ($4,800)
- [ ] **MAYBE** — Interested but needs stakeholder alignment

## SCORING GUIDE

### **CONFIRM** ✅
- Prospect says yes / in range / no objection
- Natural fit with current process
- Timeline/authority realistic
- Enthusiasm present

### **GRAY** ⚠️
- Prospect hedges / asks about terms / conditional yes
- Some concern but not a deal-killer
- Needs clarification or iteration
- Medium confidence

### **REFUTE** ❌
- Prospect says no / too expensive / out of budget
- Fundamental mismatch with need/timeline
- Would require significant repositioning
- Clear objection, not just hesitation

## HANDLING OBJECTIONS (ON-CALL PLAYBOOK)

### **"That's too expensive ($24K)"**
→ Respond: "I hear you. What would feel more in range? Or let's talk about ROI—how much do you currently spend on swag? We often pay for ourselves in 6 months."

### **"How is this different from [Competitor]?"**
→ Respond: "Great question. The key difference is [SPEED / BRAND ACCURACY / NO MOQs]. We go live in minutes vs. their weeks."

### **"I need to check with my CFO"**
→ Respond: "Totally understand. Just to clarify—is this a 'yes in principle, pending finance review' or more exploratory? I want to make sure I'm setting right expectations."

### **"We already do this with [Vendor]"**
→ Respond: "Cool, I'd love to understand your current process. What's working well? What would you change? Sometimes when I show [FEATURE], it clicks differently."

### **"Can you handle our brand guidelines?"**
→ Respond: "Absolutely. [Show demo if needed.] The Brand DNA system pulls your exact assets—colors, logos, typography. We use your official guidelines, not guesses."
