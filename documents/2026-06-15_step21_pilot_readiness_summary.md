## Key Findings (Top 5)

### 1. Warm-Intro Moat is Real (100% Attribution)
**Finding:** Every single prospect (5/5) attributed their meeting to a warm introduction. 100% said they would NOT have taken a cold email.

**Implication:** Warm introductions are our defensible GTM channel, not product differentiation. Scale through partner ecosystem (HubSpot, Workday, ADP) and investor networks, not cold outreach.

**Action:** Redirect all GTM spend from cold email/SDR to partner relationship building and referral programs.

---

### 2. Speed Value Exceeds Expectation ($7,500 vs. $5K target)
**Finding:** Prospects quantified average $7,500 annual value (150% above $5K target), spanning labor savings ($1K-4K) + strategic agility ($3K-8K).

**Implication:** Speed is the #1 ROI driver, not brand fidelity. Build business case calculators and track time-to-fulfillment metrics obsessively.

**Action:** Make "10 minutes to live store" the #1 message in all marketing and sales collateral.

---

### 3. $24K Pricing is Sweet Spot (3/5 immediate adoption)
**Finding:** 3/5 prospects chose $24K outright; 2/5 willing at $24K with ROI clarity or approval. Zero pushback on principle.

**Implication:** Price resistance is not price sensitivity—it's lack of business justification or approval friction. Build ROI calculator to address Census/Mercury objections.

**Action:** Build ROI calculator tool by June 17 for Census and Mercury conversion.

---

### 4. Onboarding is Primary, But Not Sole Use Case (41% vs. 40%)
**Finding:** Aggregate use-case mix is 41% onboarding, 36% events/appreciation, 23% recognition. 2/5 prospects weight events equally.

**Implication:** Beachhead is confirmed, but secondary and tertiary use cases are real. Multi-use-case messaging required in Year 2.

**Action:** Develop event-swag and recognition workflows in Year 2 roadmap. Position as "onboarding-first, events-second" in current messaging.

---

### 5. Storefront Delight Resonates with Buyer Persona (80% validation)
**Finding:** 4/5 prospects rated storefront delight at 7+, framing it as employee autonomy + respect signal. Resonates with People & Culture decision-makers.

**Implication:** Storefront is a meaningful differentiator for our target persona (People/Culture leaders).

**Action:** Feature storefront prominently in marketing to People & Culture buyers.

## Biggest Surprises (Learnings)

### Surprise #1: Cold Email Would Have Failed Completely
**Expected:** ~30% of prospects would take cold email  
**Reality:** 0% would take cold email; 100% would have declined  
**Implication:** Our entire GTM model must be warm-intro based. Do not invest in cold outreach.

### Surprise #2: Speed Value is Strategic, Not Just Operational
**Expected:** Speed saves 10-15 hours/year (~$1,500-2,500)  
**Reality:** Prospects quantify 20-40 hours/year + strategic agility value ($5K-10K)  
**Implication:** Speed unlocks retention and culture signals, not just time savings.

### Surprise #3: Brand-Fidelity Intensity is Higher Than Expected
**Expected:** 50% of prospects rate brand-fidelity as 8+  
**Reality:** 80% rate it as 8+; brand identity is core to early-stage hiring  
**Implication:** Design/brand ops is table-stakes, not nice-to-have.

### Surprise #4: CFO Approval Cycles Are Slower Than Expected
**Expected:** Sales leaders can approve $24K pilots independently  
**Reality:** 2/5 prospects require CFO/budget approval for pilot  
**Implication:** Sales cycle may extend 1-2 weeks for CFO sign-off.

### Surprise #5: Events/Appreciation Use Cases Are Nearly Equal to Onboarding
**Expected:** Onboarding would be 50%+ of use cases  
**Reality:** Onboarding 41%, Events/Appreciation 36%  
**Implication:** Can't market as "onboarding-only" product. Need multi-use-case positioning.
