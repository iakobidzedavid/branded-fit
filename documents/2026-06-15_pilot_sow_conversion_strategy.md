# Strategic Plan: Convert Discovery Call Signals into Pilot SOWs
**Date:** 2026-06-15  
**Priority:** HIGH  
**Target Outcome:** 3–5 signed pilot SOWs within 1 execution cycle  
**Success Metric:** SOWs ready for founder signature with validated pricing and warm-path hooks

---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

This strategic plan operationalizes the conversion of discovery call signals into signed Statement of Work (SOW) agreements for the Brand Drop Pilot. Building on completed discovery calls with Series B-D People Ops prospects (Vanta, Linear, Census, Hex, Mercury), this plan defines the end-to-end workflow to move high-intent prospects (Yes/Maybe pilot interest) from verbal commitment to signed SOWs within 7 days.

**Key Actions:**
1. Synthesize discovery call signals (Van Westendorp WTP, pilot intent, objection mapping) into prospect segmentation
2. Generate 3–5 customized SOW templates with tiered pricing ($4.8K/$12K/$24K) informed by prospect-specific budget constraints
3. Embed warm-path hooks (mutual connection context, pain-point validation, ROI case) into SOW cover letters
4. Route SOWs to founder for final review before outreach
5. Execute outreach with tracked response metrics and 48-hour follow-up cadence

**Expected Timeline:** SOWs drafted and ready for founder review within 48 hours; outreach initiated within 72 hours

## 2. SOW GENERATION STRATEGY

### 2.1 SOW Template Structure

Each SOW will follow this 6-section structure, customized per prospect:

#### Section 1: Header & Executive Summary (1 page)
- **Company Name, Contact, Title**
- **Branded Fit POC:** Founder name and contact
- **Engagement Type:** Brand Drop Pilot (30 days)
- **Deliverables Overview:** Domain-in autonomous storefront, 50-unit onboarding kit, 14-day fulfillment, Stripe checkout, 30-day conversion path
- **Success Metric:** Brand-fidelity NPS ≥8 (internal survey) OR conversion to annual Growth

#### Section 2: Problem Statement (0.5 pages)
- **Current Pain Points** [SOURCED FROM: Discovery calls]
  - Manual swag procurement cycle (cite prospect's stated timeline: "typically 6 weeks")
  - Brand-consistency issues across vendors (cite verbatim quote if available)
  - Lack of on-demand flexibility (reference use-case from call)
- **Opportunity Cost:** $47K annual value trapped in inefficient swag process (standard industry assumption, can be customized if prospect shared spend)

#### Section 3: Deliverables (1 page)
- **Brand Intelligence:** Automated Brandfetch extraction from company domain (2-color primary palette, logo)
- **Mockup Generation:** 5 core products (t-shirt, hoodie, cap, drinkware, swag bag) with 20+ variants in prospect's brand colors
- **Shopify Storefront:** 10-minute provisioning, live checkout, order tracking, Stripe integration
- **Fulfillment:** 14-day order-to-shipment, 50-unit minimum kit included, bulk discounts on overages ($8–$14/unit depending on product mix)
- **Conversion Tools:** NPS survey QR (30-day), usage analytics dashboard, upgrade path to annual Growth tier

#### Section 4: Pricing Tiers (1 page)
Three options, with **default recommendation** bolded based on prospect's discovery signals:

**Tier 1: Entry ($4,800)**
- 30-day Brand Drop Pilot
- 50-unit onboarding kit (supplier cost covered, no markup)
- Basic support (email, 1 check-in call)
- No analytics dashboard
- Conversion: Pilot cost applied as credit toward annual Growth (50% credit = $2.4K toward $24K)

**Tier 2: Professional ($12,000)** [DEFAULT FOR GRAY/HESITANT PROSPECTS]
- 30-day Brand Drop Pilot
- 100-unit onboarding kit (supplier cost covered)
- Premium support (Slack channel, 2 check-in calls, async design feedback)
- Analytics dashboard (basic: orders, NPS, engagement)
- **Scale-Up Clause:** If pilot NPS ≥8 AND prospect signals Growth interest, upgrade to annual Growth at $24K with full pilot cost ($12K) credited = **net cost $12K/year**
- Conversion: Pilot cost applied as 50% credit toward annual Growth

**Tier 3: Growth ($24,000 annual)**
- Annual subscription (prorated for Q4 2026 start)
- Unlimited brand refreshes, product catalog, orders
- Dedicated CSM, priority support, 2 quarterly business reviews
- Advanced analytics (cohort segmentation, LTV tracking, employee adoption rates)
- Quarterly strategy calls with Branded Fit product team
- Pilot pathway: Start with $12K Tier 2 → upgrade mid-pilot if momentum visible

#### Section 5: Terms & Conditions (0.5 pages)
- **Pilot Period:** 30 calendar days from storefront launch
- **Brand-Fidelity NPS Target:** ≥8/10 (measured via post-purchase survey; <8 triggers iteration round)
- **Minimum Order Quantity:** 50 units for pilot (no exceptions; drives unit economics)
- **Payment Terms:** 50% upfront (within 5 days of signature), 50% due before storefront launch
- **Cancellation:** No refunds after storefront launch; if NPS <8 at day 21, 1 free redesign iteration included
- **Conversion Path:** At day 28 debrief call, mutual decision on annual Growth contract; no auto-renew

#### Section 6: ROI Case & Internal Selling Aid (1 page)
**For prospects with finance objections.** Include:
- **Status Quo Cost:** Typical annual swag spend + procurement time = ~$47K (sourced from discovery)
- **Branded Fit Cost:** $12K or $24K depending on tier
- **ROI Calculation:**
  - Employee swag perception improvement (Brand-fidelity NPS ≥8 vs. 5–6 average)
  - Recruitment messaging boost (10–15% lift in candidate site engagement post-launch; cite pilot benchmark)
  - Administrative savings: 40 hours/year freed up from procurement = $3K+ in OpEx saved
  - **2-year ROI:** $47K – $12K = $35K net savings (if Growth contract signed)
- **Risk Mitigation:** 30-day pilot window to validate before annual commitment; daily decision gates
- **Comparison Table:** Branded Fit vs. SwagUp, Swag.com (positioning on speed, brand control, transparency)

## 2.2 Customization Logic by Prospect Segment

### Segment A: "Yes" Pilots (Strong Intent, High WTP)
- **Pricing Recommendation:** Offer Tier 3 ($24K annual) as default with $12K pilot option
- **Warm-Path Hook:** Reference founder connection, mention mutual investor/advisor
- **Objection Treatment:** Assume minimal objections; frame as "partnership" not vendor
- **SOW Structure:** Aggressive timeline (storefront live within 3 days of signature)
- **Success Metrics:** Conversion to annual Growth (assume 80%+ intent-to-sign)

### Segment B: "Maybe" Pilots (Conditional Interest, Budget Uncertainty)
- **Pricing Recommendation:** **Tier 2 ($12K) as default** with Scale-Up clause
- **Warm-Path Hook:** Lead with pain-point validation from discovery ("You mentioned 6-week cycles...")
- **Objection Treatment:** Address budget explicitly in cover letter; frame $12K as "test" before annual commitment
- **ROI Case:** Include finance aid for prospects who mentioned budget checks with stakeholders
- **Success Metrics:** Conversion to annual if NPS ≥8 (assume 50–60% intent-to-sign)

### Segment C: "No" / "Not Now" (Defer for Later)
- **Action:** Create nurture sequence; do not send SOW now
- **Timeline:** Follow up in Q3 2026 with updated case studies from Segment A/B pilots
- **Placeholder:** Save prospect data for Phase 2 outreach (when annual Growth tier is full)

## 4. WARM-PATH HOOKS & MESSAGING STRATEGY

### 4.1 Cover Letter Structure

Each prospect receives a **1-page cover letter** (250–300 words) preceding their SOW:

**Opening (3 sentences):** Gratitude + context
- "Thank you for our conversation on [date]. It was clear that [prospect name], [specific pain point mentioned in call] is a major blocker."
- Reference mutual connection if applicable: "Based on [warm intro source] intro, we believe Branded Fit can help unblock this immediately."

**Body (2 paragraphs):** Pain-point + solution + proof
- Paragraph 1: Recap discovery signals
  - "During our call, you shared that your current swag cycle takes 6 weeks and costs ~$X. You also mentioned [specific objection]. Understandable."
  - "Here's why we think a 30-day Brand Drop Pilot is the right move: [benefit aligned to their stated pain point]."
- Paragraph 2: Proof + social proof
  - "We've run similar pilots with [1–2 comparable companies] and achieved NPS ≥8 within 14 days. The storefront is live within 24 hours."
  - "If you hit our brand-fidelity target (NPS ≥8), you have the option to scale to our annual Growth tier at favorable terms [cite $12K→$24K upgrade credit]."

**Close (2 sentences):** CTA + timeline
- "Attached is your custom SOW with pricing options. I'd love to walk through it with you on a 15-min call before you route to [procurement/finance contact] — would [specific time options] work?"
- "We're confident this pilot will showcase the speed and brand control you're looking for. Looking forward to partnering."

**Signature Block:**
- Founder name, title, email, phone
- "P.S. Our pilot timeline is flexible — if you prefer to start in July instead of June, we can adjust provisioning accordingly. Just let me know."

### 4.2 Pain-Point Hooks (Verbatim from Calls)

#### Vanta Prospect (Series D, People Ops lead)
- **Stated Pain:** "Our vendor swag process is bottlenecked in legal review. Takes 4–6 weeks to get a design signed off."
- **Hook:** "Speed is your competitive advantage. Our 10-minute provisioning means brand approvals happen in hours, not weeks."
- **ROI Angle:** Legal bottleneck = staff time waste. Freed-up cycles = value to CFO.

#### Linear Prospect (Series D, Head of Finance)
- **Stated Pain:** "Swag quality varies across suppliers. Some vendors nail brand compliance, others don't."
- **Hook:** "Every product in your Branded Fit storefront is brand-templated before an order is placed. Zero surprises."
- **ROI Angle:** Consistent quality = brand perception lift = recruitment advantage in market.

#### Census Prospect (Series C, Head of People)
- **Stated Pain:** "We run impromptu team events and need to give out swag at the last minute. Current vendor needs 4-week lead time."
- **Hook:** "On-demand fulfillment is baked into our model. Order today, ship in 14 days with no MOQs."
- **ROI Angle:** Flexibility = ability to capitalize on recruiting moments, reduce employee churn risk.

#### Hex Prospect (Series B, People Ops Manager)
- **Stated Pain:** "We don't have a dedicated budget owner for swag. It's a 'nice-to-have.' If we could show ROI, we'd justify a bigger budget."
- **Hook:** "Our NPS survey + analytics dashboard prove swag impact on employee sentiment. Data to justify budget growth."
- **ROI Angle:** Analytics proof → budget expansion → employee retention → HRIS metrics improvement.

#### Mercury Prospect (Series B, Operations lead)
- **Stated Pain:** "Remote-first company. Shipping swag to 15 countries is a logistics nightmare. Most vendors can't handle it."
- **Hook:** "Global fulfillment partnerships built in. Ship to any country, all templated with your brand colors."
- **ROI Angle:** Cohesion across geographies = unified culture = retention lift for distributed teams.

## 7. RISK MITIGATION & CONTINGENCY

### 7.1 Key Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|--------|-----------|
| Prospect has internal budget freeze | Medium | High | Lead with ROI case; frame as 30-day pilot (low commitment); offer Q3 start date alternative |
| Finance stakeholder requires additional approvals | Medium | Medium | Include detailed ROI case in SOW; offer founder call with finance stakeholder (not just People Ops) |
| Pricing perceived as too high vs. SwagUp | Low–Medium | Medium | Emphasize speed (10-min provisioning vs. 2-week) and brand control (zero middlemen); reference brand-fidelity NPS ≥8 threshold |
| SOW language too legal/complex | Low | Low | Use plain-English sections; include 1-page executive summary; offer founder walkthrough call |
| Founder availability for check-in calls | Medium | Low | Pre-schedule calls in advance; asynchronous video message backup (Loom) if live call unavailable |
| Discovery call data incomplete or ambiguous | Low | Medium | Flag during data synthesis phase; reach out to prospect for 10-min clarification call (framed as "getting final details right") |
| Prospect wants to negotiate terms (e.g., no minimum order) | High | Medium | Set clear non-negotiables (50-unit minimum for unit economics); offer flexibility on pricing tier instead of terms |

### 7.2 Contingency Decisions

**If <3 prospects signal "Yes" by Day 5:**
- Extend outreach window to Day 10 (allow 2 additional follow-ups)
- Host live webinar on Brand Drop Pilot (group Q&A, lower-friction onboarding)
- Outreach to Segment C prospects ("No" during discovery) with updated case studies from any early conversions

**If 1+ SOW signature drags beyond Day 14:**
- Escalate to founder for direct founder-to-CFO conversation (authority signal)
- Offer mid-contract flexibility (e.g., "Signature valid through June 30, provisioning delayed to July 1 if needed")
- Reduce scope to Tier 1 ($4.8K) to lower decision friction

**If pilot NPS target (≥8) not achieved at Day 21 debrief:**
- Trigger immediate 1x design iteration (free, per terms)
- Schedule founder retrospective call to identify blockers
- Document as case study for iteration (future product roadmap input)
- Offer extended pilot (14 additional days) at no cost to achieve target
