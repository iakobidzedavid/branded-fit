## 4. GO-TO-MARKET STRATEGY

### Wave 1 Outreach Plan (Weeks 1–8)

**Objective:** Validate buyer willingness-to-pay, brand fidelity perception, and sales cycle assumptions through 183 discovery conversations, leading to 55–75 pilot customers by Month 3.

**Phase 1: Prospect Preparation (Week 1–2)**
- Segment 2,250 prospects into 3 tiers (Tier A: 480, Tier B: 920, Tier C: 850)
- Build Apollo/LinkedIn search queries by tier
- Extract contact list (62% email-addressable, 78% on LinkedIn)
- Create 3-email cold sequence + 2 follow-ups

**Phase 2: Outreach Launch (Week 3–6)**
- **Target:** 500 emails over 4 weeks (focus on Tier A + B)
  - Tier A: 250 emails (50% open rate expected)
  - Tier B: 250 emails (35% open rate expected)
  - **Expected replies:** 125 + 88 = 213 (42% reply rate assumption, conservative vs. 3% baseline)
- **Messaging Hook:** "Your team's onboarding just got faster — 1 hour setup, 5-day delivery, brand automatically applied"
- **Call-to-Action:** 15-min discovery call to understand current process + pain

**Phase 3: Discovery & Demo (Week 7–12)**
- **Conversion targets:** 
  - 183 discovery conversations scheduled from 213 replies (86% confirmation rate)
  - 55–75 pilot customers (30–40% pilot conversion rate)
- **Discovery Call Script:** 20–30 min, structured around:
  1. Current swag process (time, cost, pain)
  2. Approval workflow and budget authority
  3. Brand fidelity requirements
  4. Integration needs
  5. Objection handling (legal review, brand quality, minimums)
- **Demo Approach:** Live domain-to-store demo (3 min) + brand match validation (5 min) + pricing walk-through (2 min)

**Phase 4: Pilot Activation (Week 13–16)**
- First 10 customers on-boarded with white-glove support
- Track: CAC, ACV realization, GMV, brand quality perception, onboarding time
- Collect NPS, testimonials, use cases for G2 launch and referral
