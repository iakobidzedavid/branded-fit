## Executive Summary

Five customers (Vanta, Linear, Census, Hex, Mercury) have completed the Brand Drop Pilot program ($4,800 each, 14-day fulfillment) and are now using the product. This cohort represents the highest-signal validation group available: they've already invested capital, integrated the product into operations, and survived the critical onboarding window. 

The discovery call program will validate or refute six **gray assumptions** that determine GO/NO-GO on the $24K annual Growth tier pricing model and Y1 product roadmap priorities. Each 45-minute structured call will follow an identical question script to ensure comparability across all five customers. Results will be aggregated into a tally sheet (yes/no/gray per assumption) and synthesized into a founder decision memo with high-confidence recommendations.

**Success Criteria:**
- 5/5 calls completed with recording and detailed notes
- ≥4/5 customers confirm brand-fidelity NPS ≥8/10
- ≥4/5 customers confirm provisioning time ≤10 minutes
- ≥3/5 customers express yes/strong-maybe on $24K annual conversion
- 2-3 representative quotes extracted per assumption
- Tally sheet completed with yes/no/gray classifications
- Call transcripts synthesized into 1-page summary per customer

## Call Structure & Script (45 Minutes Total)

### Opening (2 minutes)
**Goal:** Build rapport, establish credibility, request recording consent.

**Script:**
> "Hi [Name], thanks so much for taking the time. I'm [Your Name] with Branded Fit. We ran the Brand Drop Pilot with your team a couple weeks ago, and we got great feedback. I wanted to spend 45 minutes diving deeper into how the experience went—what worked, what didn't, and where we should be focusing next.
> 
> I'd love to record this call so I can capture the details accurately. Is that okay with you?"

**Actions:**
- Confirm recording consent (document yes/no in tracker)
- Open Zoom recording
- Take 1-2 min for small talk (ask about onboarding swag experience, any early feedback from employees)

---

### Discovery Phase (8 minutes)
**Goal:** Establish baseline pain points and context.

**Open-Ended Discovery Questions (70% listen / 30% talk):**

1. **"Walk me through your experience with the Brand Drop Pilot from start to finish. What was the biggest surprise—either positive or negative?"**
   - *Probe:* "How did your team react when they received the swag?"
   - *Probe:* "Was there anything that felt slow or clunky?"

2. **"Beyond the swag, how are you thinking about using storefront tools like ours in the future?"**
   - *Probe:* "Are there other moments in the employee journey where branded experiences matter?"
   - *Listen for:* Use-case expansion signals (A5)

**Assumption-Mapping Notes:**
- Listen for unsolicited brand-fidelity feedback (A1)
- Capture any mentions of timeline or speed (A2)
- Flag warm-intro mentions (A4)

---

### Structured Assumption Validation (30 minutes)

**For each assumption below, ask the scripted question, listen for answer, then probe for deeper context.**

---

#### **Assumption A1: Brand-Fidelity NPS (5 min)**

**Scripted Question:**
> "On a scale of 1 to 10, how accurately do you think the generated storefront matched your brand identity?"

**Follow-Up:**
> "Walk me through what worked and what didn't. If you could change one thing about how the storefront captured your brand, what would it be?"

**Acceptance Threshold:** ≥8/10  
**Outcome Recording:** Exact number + representative quote

**Example Success Quote:**
> "The color palette and typography matched our guidelines perfectly. We only had to ask for one small tweak to the header layout."

**Example Refutation Quote:**
> "It felt like a generic template. The brand personality didn't come through—it looked corporate but not 'us.'"

---

#### **Assumption A2: Provisioning Speed (5 min)**

**Scripted Question:**
> "From the moment you submitted your domain to when you saw the first mockup render, how many minutes elapsed? Can you give me an exact number if you remember?"

**Follow-Up:**
> "How did that timeline compare to what you expected? Would you have preferred faster or slower?"

**Acceptance Threshold:** ≤10 minutes  
**Outcome Recording:** Exact minutes + perception quote

**Example Success Quote:**
> "Maybe 5-6 minutes? Way faster than I thought it would be. We were expecting to wait days."

**Example Refutation Quote:**
> "It was like 20-25 minutes. By the time the mockup loaded, I'd moved on to other stuff. It killed the momentum."

---

#### **Assumption A3: $24K Annual Growth Tier WTP (6 min)**

**Scripted Question:**
> "Imagine we offered a Growth tier: $24K/year, which includes 18,000 swag credits, access to our Brand DNA platform, and quarterly check-ins with our team. Would you sign that for your company?"

**Follow-Up on Objection:**
> "What would need to change for you to say yes? Is it the price, the feature set, or something else?"

**Follow-Up on Yes/Maybe:**
> "What's the single biggest value driver for you in that offering?"

**Acceptance Threshold:** ≥3/5 respond yes or strong maybe  
**Outcome Recording:** Y/N/Maybe + reasoning quote

**Example Yes Quote:**
> "Honestly, yes. The 18K credits alone justify it if we do quarterly refreshes. And having a dedicated contact would be huge."

**Example No Quote:**
> "Not at that price point. Maybe $12K, but $24K feels enterprise-y for us. We're still testing whether this is even a permanent program."

**Example Maybe Quote:**
> "I'd need to see the exact Brand DNA platform features. If it includes advanced targeting or compliance reporting, definitely. If it's just a database, then probably not."

---

#### **Assumption A4: Warm-Intro Conversion (3 min)**

**Scripted Question:**
> "How did you first hear about Branded Fit? Was it through a referral, a community, content, or something else?"

**Follow-Up:**
> "Who specifically introduced you, or what community/content pointed you our way?"

**Acceptance Threshold:** Document source; flag warm intros (e.g., "referred by [Name] at [Company]")  
**Outcome Recording:** Source + referrer name + perception of that channel

**Example Warm-Intro Quote:**
> "Sarah from our HR network mentioned it. She'd used you guys for their all-hands swag, so we trusted it immediately."

**Example Organic Quote:**
> "I found you on ProductHunt. Liked the positioning, so I reached out to your founder directly."

---

#### **Assumption A5: Use-Case Expansion (6 min)**

**Scripted Question:**
> "Beyond onboarding kits, what other use cases do you see for Branded Fit in your company?"

**Follow-Up:**
> "Tell me more about that. How often would you use it? Who else would be involved?"

**Follow-Up:**
> "Are there any use cases that are currently a pain point that we could solve?"

**Acceptance Threshold:** ≥3/5 identify 2+ use cases beyond onboarding  
**Outcome Recording:** Use cases listed + frequency + budget signal

**Example Expansion Quote:**
> "For sure: all-hands meeting swag, new customer onboarding packages, sales kickoff gifts, employee referral bonuses. We do 4 big events a year, so this could be quarterly."

**Example Narrow Quote:**
> "Honestly, just onboarding. That's really our main pain point. I can't think of other places we'd use it."

---

#### **Assumption A6: Storefront Impact (5 min)**

**Scripted Question:**
> "After the pilot, how has the storefront experience changed employee perception of the brand or the onboarding experience?"

**Follow-Up:**
> "Have you gotten any specific feedback from employees about the personalized storefront vs. generic swag?"

**Follow-Up:**
> "If you had to give that improvement an NPS score (0-10, where 10 is 'transformed our onboarding'), what would it be?"

**Acceptance Threshold:** ≥3/5 report NPS lift (≥7/10) or clear qualitative improvement  
**Outcome Recording:** NPS lift + representative quote

**Example Strong Quote:**
> "Employees said it felt way more 'us.' They were actually excited to show it to their friends, not just tossing swag in a drawer. I'd call it a 9/10 improvement."

**Example Weak Quote:**
> "It was nice, but honestly not much different from what we'd get from a vendor. Employees were happy, but it wasn't transformative. Maybe a 5/10."

---

### Objection Capture & Probing (3 minutes)

**Script:**
> "Before we wrap, are there any concerns or objections you have about Branded Fit or the pilot that we should address?"

**Listen For & Record:**
- Top 3 objections (price, feature gaps, onboarding friction, integration complexity, etc.)
- Deal-breaker vs. "nice-to-have" classification
- Suggested remediation from customer

**Example Objections:**
- "The mockup approval process felt slow—we wanted faster iterations."
- "No integration with Workday, so we had to manually manage employee data."
- "The swag quality was good, but shipping delays happened twice."

## Quality Standards & Guardrails

### Data Integrity
- **No fabricated responses:** All quotes and scores come from recorded calls or detailed notes; never invented
- **Verbatim quote requirement:** Every assertion is backed by at least one direct quote from customer
- **Provenance tracking:** Every response tagged with [FROM_CALL: Customer Name, Date]

### Recording Consent & Privacy
- **Explicit consent:** Every call opens with recording request; "recording declined" noted in header if applicable
- **Secure storage:** Zoom recordings stored in password-protected folder; shared only with founder + transcriber
- **Anonymization:** If customer requests anonymization, honor it; use generic identifiers in public-facing tally sheet

### Assumption Validation Rigor
- **Gray zone definition:** Response is GRAY if it's ambiguous, contradicts other signals, or falls in middle 4-7 range on any 1-10 scale
- **No double-counting:** Each customer counts once per assumption, regardless of call duration
- **Confidence scoring:** Based on threshold, not gut feel; e.g., "3/5 yes = GRAY" per framework above
