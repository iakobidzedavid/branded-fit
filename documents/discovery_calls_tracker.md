## Data Quality Notes

**Important:** This tracker must be completed with actual prospect data, not estimated or placeholder information. Each field should reflect what was actually said or observed during the call, not what you expect or assume the prospect would say.

- Do NOT fabricate WTP responses or objections
- Do NOT invent quotes; only capture verbatim language (or note "paraphrased" if exact quote unavailable)
- Do NOT round pilot intent signals; log exactly what the prospect said (Yes/No/Maybe + confidence)
- Do NOT skip DMU or prospect context; this informs SOW negotiation and pilot execution
