Maya evaluates every swag vendor on two criteria, in order:

1. **Time-to-live storefront** — how fast can she share a branded, orderable URL with her team?
2. **On-brand redemption rate** — what % of employees actually redeem/wear the swag within 30 days?

**Branded Fit is alone in the upper-left winning quadrant: fast AND high redemption.** Every incumbent occupies either a slow lane (agencies, DIY) or a low-redemption lane (managed platforms, gifting tools) — none has cracked both simultaneously.

---

## Why No Incumbent Can Close the Gap

**Speed gap is architectural, not operational.**
SwagUp's "store in a minute" creates an empty URL container. Filling it with branded product mockups requires their design services team: a 1–3 day human queue that is the product, not a bottleneck. Branded Fit's AI pipeline (Brandfetch → LLM → Brand→Product Fit Graph → mockup renderer → Shopify) runs end-to-end in 8 minutes. SwagUp would need to rebuild their product and retire their design revenue stream — an 18-month minimum rebuild. (As of June 2026: no AI storefront roadmap disclosed.)

**Redemption gap compounds with data.**
Branded Fit's Core is the **Brand→Product Fit Graph (BPFG)** — a proprietary ML model trained on closed-loop redemption outcomes. It maps brand identity + team context → optimal SKU selection, improving with every storefront. Agencies achieve 78% redemption via skilled account managers who ask the right questions — but at 4-week timelines and $10K+ minimums. Branded Fit reaches agency-level redemption quality at self-serve speed, and the lead widens with each customer: at 200 customers / 1,000 storefronts, competitors cannot replicate without years of proprietary data.
