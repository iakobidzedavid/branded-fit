## Executive Summary

This document serves as the structured tracker for executing and documenting discovery calls with responding Step-9 prospects (Vanta, Linear, Census, Hex, Mercury, or others who have replied to warm outreach). Each call validates 6 critical business assumptions using a standardized 20-minute framework.

**Key Metrics to Capture:**
- Brand-fidelity perception (A1): NPS ≥8 target
- Provisioning-speed perception (A2): ≥40 hrs/month saved = $5K+ value
- $24K Growth-tier WTP (A3): ≥3/5 choose $24K+
- Warm-intro conversion (A4): ≥80% attribute to warm path
- Use-case fit (A5): Onboarding ≥40% of use-case mix
- Storefront impact (A6): NPS ≥7 for delight lift

## Call Script: Structured 20-Minute Agenda

### OPENING (0-2 minutes)
**Tone:** Warm, consultative, peer-to-peer founder energy

```
[GREETING]
"Hi [Name]! Thanks for making time. I know you're busy, so I want to 
make this valuable for you. We've got about 20 minutes—how much time 
do you have?"

[CREDIBILITY & WARM PATH]
"I know [mutual contact] recommended we talk. We've been working with 
a few People Ops teams on branded swag, and we're learning a lot about 
what actually moves the needle for onboarding."

[CALL GOAL]
"Today I want to show you how our platform works and get your honest 
feedback on a few things we're testing. Sound good?"

[ASK PERMISSION]
"Is now still a good time?"
```

---

### DISCOVERY (2-8 minutes)
**Tone:** Active listening (70% listen, 30% talk). Map responses to 3 pillars: Speed, Brand Fidelity, On-Demand Fulfillment.

**Open-ended questions (ask all 3-5):**

1. **Current swag process:**
   "Walk me through how you currently handle branded swag for new hires. 
   What does that process look like today?"
   
   *Listen for:* Timeline, pain points, current vendor, cost, fulfillment delays

2. **Top pain points:**
   "What's the biggest headache in that process?"
   
   *Listen for:* Speed, quality, approval cycles, budget constraints

3. **Success metrics:**
   "How do you measure success with swag? What would change your mind?"
   
   *Listen for:* Employee delight, retention signal, brand reinforcement, budget efficiency

4. **Timeline & urgency:**
   "When would you want to pilot something new? What's your timeline?"
   
   *Listen for:* Q2/Q3 budget, hiring ramp-up, event dates

5. **Peer influence:**
   "Have you talked to [peer company] about this? What's your sense?"
   
   *Listen for:* Warm-path validation, social proof hunger

**Capture:** Pain-point keywords, timeline, success definition, objections

---

### POSITIONING (8-12 minutes)
**Tone:** Consultative. Anchor to their pain points, not features.

```
[CONTEXT]
"Based on what you said, it sounds like [pain point A] and [pain point B] 
are the biggest issues. Here's what we're seeing with other teams…"

[90-SECOND DEMO]
"Let me show you what this looks like in practice. You give us a domain—
say, [company.com]—and we extract your brand automatically. Then we generate 
mockups in real time."

[SHOW DEMO: typed domain → brand extraction → mockup preview]

"That whole flow is about 10 minutes end-to-end. The mockup shows size and 
color options. They approve, and we send swag within 14 days."

[ANCHOR TO PAIN POINTS]
"So if [pain point] was a concern, this solves it by [specific mechanism]. 
And because it's on-demand, you only order what you need."

[POSITIONING STATEMENT]
"We're not trying to replace your current vendor. We're filling a specific 
gap: the ability to get branded swag *fast* and *customized* to your 
employee preferences."
```

**Show:** Live domain input → brand extraction output → mockup preview

---

### ASSUMPTION VALIDATION (12-17 minutes)
**Framework:** Ask all 6 questions in order. Score each on 1-5 scale or Yes/No.

#### **A1: Brand-Fidelity Perception**
**Question:** "On a scale of 1–10, how important is it that the mockup matches your brand guidelines exactly before you approve a bulk order?"

**Why it matters:** If brand fidelity is table-stakes (≥8), we need pixel-perfect matching.

**Score:**
- ✅ 8-10: **CONFIRMED** — Brand fidelity is critical
- ⚠️ 5-7: **GRAY** — Nice-to-have but not deal-breaker
- ❌ 1-4: **REFUTE** — Speed matters more than perfect match

**Probe:** "What would you change on the mockup?" / "Would a 95% match work?"

---

#### **A2: Provisioning-Speed Perception**
**Question:** "How much time would you save if you could go from domain to a live branded store in 10 minutes instead of 6 weeks? Can you quantify that in hours per month or annual savings?"

**Why it matters:** Speed is our #1 differentiation. If prospect can't quantify value, adoption friction is higher.

**Score & Calculation:**
- Ask: hours saved per month × hours/FTE × annual salary
- Typical: 3-5 hours/month saved = $3–8K annual value
- ✅ **CONFIRMED:** ≥$5K annual value recognized
- ⚠️ **GRAY:** $2–5K annual value
- ❌ **REFUTE:** <$2K annual value or no clear time savings

**Probe:** "How many swag orders per month?" / "Who currently manages this?" / "How much did the last swag order take from launch to delivery?"

---

#### **A3: $24K Growth-Tier WTP (Willingness to Pay)**
**Question:** "We're testing pricing for a 6-month pilot. I'll show you three anchors. Which of these feels right for your team?"

**Present Van Westendorp Pricing Ladder:**
- **Low anchor:** $12K (500 credits/month)
- **Sweet spot:** $24K (1,000 credits/month)
- **Premium:** $36K (1,500 credits/month)

"Which of these feels like the right investment for a 6-month pilot?"

**Why it matters:** Validates our beachhead pricing hypothesis.

**Score:**
- ✅ **CONFIRMED:** Prospect chooses $24K or $36K
- ⚠️ **GRAY:** Prospect chooses $12K but sees value above that
- ❌ **REFUTE:** Prospect pushes below $12K or unsure

**Probe:** "What would make $24K feel right?" / "What features would justify the step up?"

---

#### **A4: Warm-Intro Conversion**
**Question:** "How did you hear about us? And be honest—would you have taken this call if it came as a cold email from someone you didn't know?"

**Why it matters:** Validates warm-path GTM efficiency.

**Score:**
- ✅ **CONFIRMED:** Prospect attributes to warm intro AND says they wouldn't have taken cold email
- ⚠️ **GRAY:** Attributes to warm intro but unsure about cold email response
- ❌ **REFUTE:** Would have taken cold email, or no warm-intro attribution

**Probe:** "What was it about [warm contact]'s recommendation that made you jump on this?"

---

#### **A5: Use-Case Fit**
**Question:** "What are your top 3 use cases for branded swag in the next 12 months?"

*Listen and rank by frequency mentioned.*

"If you had to pick one primary use case, what would it be?"

**Why it matters:** Validates onboarding-kit as primary use case (our beachhead).

**Score & Calculation:**
- Tally each use case: onboarding, events, milestones, recognition, other
- ✅ **CONFIRMED:** Onboarding ≥40% of stated use cases
- ⚠️ **GRAY:** Onboarding 25-40% of use cases
- ❌ **REFUTE:** Onboarding <25% or not mentioned

**Probe:** "Why is [use case] #1?" / "How many onboarding kits per quarter?"

---

#### **A6: Storefront Impact Perception**
**Question:** "If your new hires could self-select colors and sizes from a branded store instead of receiving a pre-packed kit, how would that change the experience? Give me a score from 1-10 on the delight lift."

**Why it matters:** Validates employee delight as a retention lever.

**Score:**
- ✅ **CONFIRMED:** 7-10 ("Major game-changer" / "Huge delight lift")
- ⚠️ **GRAY:** 5-6 ("Nice feature, but not a big deal")
- ❌ **REFUTE:** 1-4 ("Doesn't matter much" / "Overkill")

**Probe:** "Why would it matter?" / "What would employees value?"

---

### CLOSE (17-20 minutes)
**Tone:** Consultative. Prospect owns next move.

```
[SYNTHESIS]
"So from what I'm hearing, [pain point A] and [pain point B] are your 
priorities. And if we could solve those in [timeline], that would free up 
[name]'s time for bigger-picture stuff. Is that fair?"

[NEXT STEP MENU]
"Here's what happens next. You have three options:

1. **Brand Drop Pilot** — 14 days, one onboarding kit, full feedback. 
   $4,800, no contract. You place an order, we ship in 14 days.

2. **Discovery Call 2** — If you want to dig deeper, I'm happy to 
   schedule another call with your team to answer specific questions.

3. **Peer Intro** — If this doesn't feel right, I can intro you to 
   [peer company] who's using this. Maybe talking to them shifts things."

[PILOT INTEREST GAUGE]
"Which of those feels right? And what would need to be true for 
option 1 [Brand Drop Pilot] to happen?"

[EXPECTATIONS SETTING]
"If you decide to do the pilot:
- We ship branded swag in 14 days.
- You get a 30-day NPS survey to see how employees react.
- Then we debrief and talk about a full-year plan.
- No pressure—this is just testing."

[PROSPECT OWNS NEXT MOVE]
"What feels doable for you? And when?"
```

**Prospect Response Classification:**
- ✅ **PILOT YES:** "Let's do it" / "In for the pilot" → Move to SOW
- ⚠️ **PILOT MAYBE:** "I need to check with [peer]" / "Let me think" → Follow-up in 48h
- ❌ **PILOT NO:** "Not a fit" / "Don't have budget" → Document objection, offer peer intro

## Appendix: Call Recording & Transcript Best Practices

### Recording Setup
- **Tool:** Zoom, Google Meet, or Loom (record with permission)
- **Quality:** Audio + video (capture prospect reactions)
- **Storage:** Shared Google Drive folder with timestamp naming
- **Naming:** `YYYY-MM-DD_[CompanyName]_DiscoveryCall_[YourName].mp4`

### Transcription
- **Method:** Otter.ai, Rev.com, or manual notes
- **Timing:** Complete within 24h of call
- **Format:** [Timestamp] Speaker: Quote
- **Review:** Identify key assumptions quotes

### Data Protection
- **NDA:** Ensure recording consent documented
- **Storage:** Restricted access (founder + sales team only)
- **Retention:** Archive after 90 days (per compliance)

## FAQs & Troubleshooting

### Q: What if prospect doesn't show up?
**A:** Document as "No-show" + no-answer email follow-up within 2h. Reschedule within 48h. Don't count toward 5-call minimum.

### Q: What if prospect doesn't want to be recorded?
**A:** Take detailed notes instead. Still counts toward 5 calls. Transcribe notes within 1h of call end.

### Q: What if prospect gives conflicting signals (loves speed, won't pay for it)?
**A:** Score as GRAY. Capture exact quote. This is valuable data for pricing/positioning.

### Q: Should I mention competitors (SwagUp, Bonusly)?
**A:** Only if prospect brings them up. Then ask: "What's missing with [competitor]?" Don't bash them.

### Q: What if they want a pilot but can't commit budget?
**A:** Move to MAYBE category. Offer: "Let's do a $4,800 Brand Drop Pilot first. Then we talk annual." Get timeline for decision.
