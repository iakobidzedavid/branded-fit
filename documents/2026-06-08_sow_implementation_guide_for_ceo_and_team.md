## 4. SIGNATURE & PAYMENT WORKFLOW

### SIGNATURE METHOD OPTIONS

**Option A: DocuSign (Recommended for Speed)**
- Setup: CEO uploads SOW + Cover Letter to DocuSign template
- Process: DocuSign emails prospect with signature request
- Timeline: Prospect signs within 24-48 hours (typically)
- Files: Signed PDFs auto-stored in DocuSign, forwarded to CEO/Ops email
- Cost: DocuSign free tier (3 envelopes/month) or $10/month
- Recommendation: **Use this** for fastest execution

**Option B: HelloSign (Alternative)**
- Setup: CEO uploads documents, sends signature request
- Process: Prospect signs via link, DocuSign eSignature backend
- Timeline: 24-48 hours
- Cost: ~$10-25/month
- Recommendation: Similar to DocuSign; use if already integrated with company tech stack

**Option C: Print + Physical Signature + Scan**
- Setup: Print SOW + Cover Letter on letterhead
- Process: CEO signs, ship to prospect (FedEx or courier), prospect signs, scan back
- Timeline: 3-5 business days (slower)
- Storage: Scan to PDF, file in secure folder
- Recommendation: Use only if prospect requests (older companies, legal requirement)

**Option D: Print + Email Scan**
- Setup: Print, CEO signs, scan to PDF
- Process: Email PDF to prospect, prospect prints, signs, emails back scanned copy
- Timeline: 2-3 days
- Storage: File PDF in secure folder
- Recommendation: Fallback if prospect has no eSignature capability

## 8. FAQ & TROUBLESHOOTING

### Q1: What if a prospect signs the SOW but doesn't pay within 7 days?

**A:** Follow-up sequence:
- **Day 7:** Send friendly payment reminder (invoice + payment link)
- **Day 10:** Call prospect directly, confirm they received invoice, address any payment concerns
- **Day 14:** If still unpaid, send final reminder: "To begin your pilot on [scheduled date], we need payment by [final date]. Otherwise, we'll need to reschedule."
- **Day 21:** If unpaid, mark contract null/void. Offer to reschedule when ready to pay.

**Note:** Payment is the trigger to begin Pilot Day 1. No payment = no work starts.

---

### Q2: What if a prospect wants to modify the SOW (e.g., lower price, longer timeline, different scope)?

**A:** Follow change order process:
1. **Document request:** "So you'd like to extend the pilot from 30 to 45 days. Let me confirm: does that impact the $4,800 fee, or is it the same?"
2. **Create change order:** Provide written amendment with new terms, price (if applicable), timeline
3. **Get signatures:** Both parties sign change order; it becomes binding amendment to SOW
4. **Update internal tracking:** Note any changes to timeline, deliverables, or pricing

**Default response to price reductions:** "We've priced the pilot at $4,800 to cover our costs (brand extraction, storefront design, 50-unit fulfillment, analytics setup). We can't reduce price, but we could explore: (a) 50-unit kit in fewer products, (b) shorter timeline (15 days instead of 30), or (c) standby status to begin when you're ready."

---

### Q3: What if we miss a delivery deadline (e.g., Brand Charter is late on Day 2)?

**A:** SOW allows prospect to request **50% refund for each missed deliverable**.

**Mitigation:**
1. **Communication:** Proactively notify prospect ASAP if a delay is anticipated (don't let them discover it)
2. **Root cause:** Explain why (e.g., "We're waiting for your logo files; once we have them, Brand Charter will be ready same day")
3. **New ETA:** Provide revised delivery date
4. **Goodwill:** Offer bonus (e.g., +1 week of analytics post-pilot, or $500 product credit toward Growth contract)
5. **Avoid refund:** Most prospects accept this; refund risk is low IF you communicate early and resolve quickly

**Red flag:** If you miss >1 deadline, conversion to Growth contract is at risk. Prioritize on-time delivery.

---

### Q4: What if the prospect's NPS score is 5 or 6 (not >7, but not terrible)?

**A:** Conversion criteria require NPS >7. However, **5-6 is a "passive"** and valuable feedback source.

**Approach:**
1. **Understanding:** "Your score of [6] tells us you value the speed and brand accuracy, but there's something we could do better. What is it?"
2. **Specific ask:** "If we [fixed X], would you recommend us to a peer?"
3. **Conditional offer:** "Your pilot met 2 of 3 success metrics. If we commit to [improvement], would you consider a 3-month Growth pilot at $9,600 ($24K / 2.5) to test the improvements?"

**Note:** Only expand to Growth contract if NPS >7 per SOW terms. Anything less = debrief + learning, not automatic expansion.

---

### Q5: What if a prospect wants their storefront to remain live after the pilot (post-Day 30)?

**A:** Great question! Default terms:

- **During pilot (Days 1-30):** Storefront is live on Branded Fit infrastructure (included in $4,800)
- **Post-pilot (Day 30+):** 
  - **If converting to Growth contract:** Storefront stays live, managed under Growth contract
  - **If NOT converting:** Storefront is archived on Day 31; prospect can request a data export of customer list + orders, but live storefront is shut down

**Alternative:** Offer to transfer storefront to prospect's own Shopify account (one-time $500 data migration fee). This gives prospect ownership post-pilot even if they don't convert to Growth.

---

### Q6: What if a prospect reports technical issues during the pilot (e.g., Stripe not working, Shopify connection broken)?

**A:** Triage and escalate:

1. **Severity:** Is the storefront completely down (critical), or just slow (medium)?
2. **Cause:** Is it a Branded Fit issue or third-party (Stripe, Shopify, Printify)?
3. **Resolution:**
   - **Branded Fit issue:** Fix immediately (SLA: within 4 business hours)
   - **Third-party issue:** Contact support, provide ETA, keep prospect informed
4. **Communication:** Update prospect daily via email or Slack until resolved
5. **Impact on metrics:** If technical outage lasts >24 hours and impacts Provisioning Time or Analytics data, consider extending pilot by same duration OR offer goodwill credit toward Growth contract

**Key:** Do NOT let technical issues go unaddressed. They directly threaten conversion to Growth contract.

---

### Q7: Can we launch all 3 pilots simultaneously, or should we stagger?

**A:** **Recommend staggering by 2-3 days:**

- **Pilot A:** Start June 10
- **Pilot B:** Start June 12-13
- **Pilot C:** Start June 14-15

**Reason:** 
- Allows team to troubleshoot issues on Pilot A before repeating same work for B and C
- Reduces risk of simultaneous failures
- Staggered Day 30 completion dates (July 10, 12, 14) allow CEO to focus on 1 expansion call per day

**If all succeed and convert, you're running 3 Growth contracts starting in staggered fashion, which is cleaner operationally.**

### Q8: What should we do with the data after the pilot ends?

**A:** Data ownership is critical; handle per SOW terms:

1. **Customer data (orders, emails, purchases):** Prospect owns; Branded Fit deletes from analytics DB on Day 31 unless Growth contract is signed
2. **Storefront design (brand colors, layouts, product selections):** Prospect owns; retained only if Growth contract signed
3. **Performance data (analytics, metrics, NPS):** Branded Fit retains for case study purposes (with prospect's written consent)
4. **Learnings (technical improvements, brand extraction refinements):** Branded Fit retains and uses to improve product for other customers

**Post-Pilot Data Export:** If prospect doesn't convert to Growth, offer to export:
- Customer list (names, emails, order amounts)
- Product performance report (top sellers, conversion rates)
- Analytics summary (4-week overview in PDF)

Cost: Included in pilot fee (no extra charge).
