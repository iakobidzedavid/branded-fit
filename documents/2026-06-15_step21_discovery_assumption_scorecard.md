## APPENDIX: Discovery Call Framework Summary

### Call Structure (45–60 min):
1. **Warm Intro (5 min):** Credibility + context + permission
2. **Current-State Discovery (10 min):** Pain points, timeline, current spend
3. **Live Demo (7 min):** Command Console with their domain
4. **Assumption Validation (30 min):** 6 assumptions scored + objection exploration
5. **Objection Handling (5 min):** Top concerns + counters
6. **Close (3 min):** Next step (pilot SOW / follow-up call)

### 29-Field Tracker (Completed per Call):
✅ Prospect name, company, title, email, phone  
✅ Current swag process, timeline, annual budget  
✅ Pain intensity (1–5 scale)  
✅ Brand-fidelity rating (1–5 scale)  
✅ Demo reaction (impressed/interested/skeptical)  
✅ WTP at $2K/$4.8K/$24K/$36K anchors  
✅ Signing authority (DMU, approval timeline)  
✅ Use cases identified (count + categories)  
✅ Objections raised (open text)  
✅ Pilot intent (yes/maybe/no)  
✅ Confidence score (high/medium/low)  
✅ Next step agreed + timeline  
✅ Notes (follow-up items, special conditions)  

### Scoring Methodology:
- **Assumption Status:** CONFIRMED (≥threshold), GRAY (mixed), REFUTE (<threshold)
- **Confidence:** HIGH (3/3 assumed), MEDIUM (2/3), LOW (≤1/3)
- **Recommendation:** GO (3+ CONFIRMED), GO-CONTINGENT (2 CONFIRMED + 1 GRAY + clear path), HOLD (≥2 REFUTE)
