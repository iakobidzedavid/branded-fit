# STATEMENT OF WORK
## Branded Fit 30-Day Brand Drop Pilot Program

**Dated:** June 8, 2026  
**Effective Date:** Upon Execution  
**Client:** Linear, Inc.  
**Service Provider:** Branded Fit  

---

## 1. SCOPE OF WORK

Branded Fit will deliver a complete 30-day **Brand Drop Pilot** consisting of:

### 1.1 Autonomous Store Generation
- Branded Fit will deploy an AI-powered domain-in Shopify storefront using Linear's corporate domain and brand assets (logo, colors, messaging)
- Brand extraction via Brandfetch API; design automated via AI; fulfillment integration via Printify
- Storefront will be live and functional within 2 business days of contract execution
- Domain will feature Linear-branded merchandise catalog (t-shirts, hoodies, hats, apparel)

### 1.2 Onboarding Kit Mockup Approval
- Branded Fit will generate 50-unit branded apparel mockup set (physical samples) for Linear approval
- Linear may request up to 2 design revisions within 5 business days of mockup delivery
- Approval deadline: Day 5 of pilot program (strict SLA for on-time fulfillment)

### 1.3 Fulfillment & Shipping
- Upon mockup approval, Branded Fit will fulfill and ship 50 units of branded merchandise to Linear's specified address
- Fulfillment timeline: 14 business days from approval date
- Shipping method: Standard ground shipping (2-5 business days)
- Linear will receive tracking information and merchandise by Day 19 of pilot

### 1.4 Admin Dashboard Access
- Linear will receive 30-day access to Branded Fit's admin dashboard
- Dashboard features:
  - Real-time storefront traffic & conversion analytics
  - Order tracking and fulfillment status
  - Brand compliance reporting
  - Pilot performance metrics (NPS, engagement, ROI)

### 1.5 Post-Pilot NPS Survey
- Branded Fit will conduct a quantitative NPS survey with Linear stakeholders (target: Linear employees + leadership team)
- Survey will measure satisfaction with brand fidelity, product quality, delivery timeliness, and overall pilot experience
- Results will be delivered within 3 business days of pilot end date

## 4. SUCCESS METRICS & CONVERSION CRITERIA

**Pilot Success = 3 Conditions Met:**

1. **Mockup Approval SLA:** Linear approves mockup design within 5 business days ✓
2. **Delivery On-Time:** Merchandise delivered to Linear's address by Day 24 ✓
3. **Pilot NPS ≥ 7:** Post-pilot survey returns average NPS score of 7 or higher (0-10 scale) ✓

**If all 3 conditions are met:** Linear qualifies for **Growth Contract Conversion** (see Section 5).

## 5. CONVERSION TO ANNUAL GROWTH CONTRACT

If the Pilot is successful (all 3 metrics met), Branded Fit will offer Linear an **optional** annual Growth Contract:

- **Annual Commitment:** $24,000 (prorated from Day 30 onward)
- **Service Includes:**
  - Unlimited storefront updates & brand refreshes
  - Quarterly merchandise product drops (4x/year new SKUs)
  - Enhanced admin dashboard with cohort analytics

  - Priority fulfillment SLA (7-day turnaround)

- **Conversion Decision Deadline:** Day 60 (30 days after Pilot ends)
- **Either party may decline conversion with no penalty or obligation**

## 6. TERMINATION & REFUND POLICY

### 6.1 Termination for Cause
- **Branded Fit Failure:** If storefront is not live by Day 5, or merchandise not fulfilled by Day 21, Linear may terminate and receive full refund of fees paid to date.
- **Linear Breach:** If Linear fails to approve mockup by Day 5 without documented cause, Branded Fit may bill for extended design services at $200/hour.

### 6.2 Termination for Convenience
- Either party may terminate after Day 5 (post-approval) by written notice
- Linear remains liable for merchandise fulfillment costs incurred up to termination date
- Branded Fit will refund unused fees (prorated daily)

### 6.3 Post-Pilot Termination
- If Linear declines Growth Contract conversion at Day 60, the engagement ends with no further obligation
- Linear retains 30-day access to admin dashboard; access reverts on Day 61
- Storefront remains live for archival purposes; Linear may request full migration to own infrastructure within 30 days
