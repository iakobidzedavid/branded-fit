## Executive Summary

Branded Fit's beachhead market (Series B–D tech, People Ops buyer) supports a unit economics profile that reaches Rule of 40 (growth + payback reciprocal) by Month 18–24, driven by a strong LTV:CAC ratio and rapid payback. The core economic engine is:

- **Annual Customer Value (ACV):** $31.5K (= $18K SaaS + 15% take-rate on $90K GMV)
- **Gross Margin:** 72% ($22.7K per customer annually)
- **CAC (blended):** $8,400 (payback in 4.5 months)
- **LTV (3-year, 40% annual retention):** $63.6K (LTV:CAC = 7.6x)
- **Rule of 40 (Month 18):** 65% growth + 22% payback reciprocal = **87% combined score**

**Pricing is sustainable at $18K/year SaaS tier for this buyer.** People Ops at Series B–D tech companies can approve $18K autonomously; competitive set (SwagUp: $1,188/year, Printfection: $5,988/year) is fragmented; and willingness-to-pay validation through 3–5 discovery calls is the highest-priority de-risk.

## 7. Sensitivity Analysis

### 7.1 Two-Way Sensitivity: CAC vs. Growth Rate

**How changes in CAC and customer acquisition rate impact $10M ARR milestone:**

```
Months to $10M ARR (lower = better)

                  CAC Payback (months)
                  3.0    3.5    4.5    5.5
Customers/mo  
12              20     21     23     26
10              22     24     26     29
8               27     30     34     38  ← Base case (8 cust/mo, 5.1mo payback = 34 mo)
6               35     38     44     49
4               51     56     67     79
```

**Key insight:** The model is more sensitive to customer acquisition rate than to CAC. Increasing customers/month from 8 to 10 (25% lift) reduces time to $10M by 8 months. Increasing CAC from $8.4K to $10K (19% lift) increases time by only 4 months.

**Action:** Prioritize sales execution and PMF validation over CAC optimization in Year 1.

### 7.2 Gross Margin Sensitivity

**How changes in COGS structure impact contribution margin and payback:**

| Scenario | COGS % of GMV | Gross Margin | Contribution/customer | Payback (months) |

| **Base** | 20% | 72% | $22.7K | 5.1 |
| **Optimistic** (Scale negotiation + alternative supplier) | 17% | 75% | $23.6K | 4.8 |

**Implication:** Gross margin compression from 72% to 65% (worst case) increases payback from 5.1 to 5.5 months—a manageable 8% change. Focus on retention and expansion is higher-impact than squeezing another 2% margin.

### 7.3 Retention Sensitivity

### 11.2 Decision Tree: If Key Assumptions Fail

- Impact: LTV drops from $63.6K to $56K, reducing venture upside; compounds with slower growth if churn is due to weak PMF.
