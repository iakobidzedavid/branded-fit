## The 3-Tier Segmentation

### Tier A: High-Fit (480 companies | 21%)
**Best for launching Wave 1**
- Series B companies | 80–200 FTE | 50–100% growth
- Buyer: People Ops Manager with $18K–$50K autonomous authority
- Pain: "Hiring 15–25/quarter; need branded swag in minutes not weeks"
- Sales cycle: 2–4 weeks
- Win rate: 40–50%
- Expected meetings from 480 outreach: 48–72
- ARR potential: ~$315K

### Tier B: Medium-Fit (920 companies | 41%)
**Largest segment; vendor consolidation angle**
- Series B–C | 150–350 FTE | 25–50% growth
- Buyer: Head of People (VP escalation for >$25K)
- Pain: "3 swag vendors, no brand consistency, no spend visibility"
- Sales cycle: 4–8 weeks
- Win rate: 25–35%
- Expected meetings: 55–92
- ARR potential: ~$432K

### Tier C: Expansion-Fit (850 companies | 38%)
**Enterprise-grade; longer sales cycle; highest contract value**
- Series C–D | 300–500+ FTE | <30% growth
- Buyer: CPO / VP People with $25K–$100K+ authority
- Pain: "500+ employees; need one platform for all engagement"
- Sales cycle: 8–16 weeks
- Win rate: 15–25%
- Expected meetings: 42–68
- ARR potential: ~$306K

**Total Blended: 183 expected qualified meetings | 55–75 closed customers | $1.05M ARR (15-month window)**

## Buyer Profile Summary

### Top Titles (In Order of Frequency)
1. **People Ops Manager** (35%) — Day-to-day ops, autonomous up to $25K
2. **Head of Employee Experience** (28%) — Strategic lead, $25K–$50K authority
3. **HR Manager** (22%) — Junior coordinator, escalates >$5K
4. **Chief People Officer** (10%) — Executive-level, $25K–$100K+ authority
5. **VP Operations** (5%) — Owns swag at early-stage; delegates to People team

### Decision Authority
- **<$15K:** Autonomous (People Ops Manager)
- **$15K–$50K:** Autonomous or 1-level escalation (VP People)
- **$50K–$100K:** Multi-stakeholder (VP + Finance)
- **>$100K:** Board/Annual budget cycle

## Outreach Strategy by Tier

- **Channels:** 60% LinkedIn DM | 30% cold email | 10% warm intro
- **Hook:** "Go from domain → live branded storefront in 90 seconds"
- **Expected reply:** 10–15% (brand-aware, high-intent buyers)
- **Timeline:** 2–4 weeks to meeting
- **Volume:** 480 companies × 12% reply = 58 qualified meetings
- **Email template:** See Appendix B in full segmentation doc

### Tier B: Vendor Consolidation Angle
- **Channels:** 50% cold email | 35% LinkedIn | 15% warm intro
- **Hook:** "One dashboard for all 3+ swag vendors + brand consistency"
- **Expected reply:** 6–10% (multi-touch sequence needed)
- **Timeline:** 4–8 weeks to meeting
- **Volume:** 920 companies × 8% reply = 74 qualified meetings

### Tier C: Executive-Driven, Content-First
- **Channels:** 40% warm intro | 35% LinkedIn | 25% content + demand gen
- **Hook:** "Enterprise swag + recognition platform for 500+ distributed teams"
- **Expected reply:** 5–8% (long sales cycle, CPO approval chains)
- **Timeline:** 8–16 weeks to meeting
- **Volume:** 850 companies × 6% reply = 51 qualified meetings
